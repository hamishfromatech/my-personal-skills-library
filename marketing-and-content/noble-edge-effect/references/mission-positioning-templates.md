## Anti-Pattern Detection Checklist
Before publishing any mission-related content, run this check:

- [ ] Is the social good structurally integrated into the product? (Not a donation, not a side page.)
- [ ] Is there specific, verifiable evidence? (Not "we care about privacy" but "here's the exact data we collect: none.")
- [ ] Is the mission discoverable after competence is established? (Not the first impression unless the user arrived via mission search.)
- [ ] Does the content avoid guilt or shame? (Positive framing only.)
- [ ] Would the content still be true if we were not trying to sell anything? (Authenticity test.)
- [ ] Is the contrast frame fair and factual? (No straw-manning competitors.)
