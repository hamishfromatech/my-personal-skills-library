# Neuromarketing AI Personalization Ethics 2026 — Evidence Base

## Source 1: Frontiers in Neuroergonomics (2025)

**Citation:** Gupta, R., Kapoor, A.P., & Verma, H.V. (2025). "Neuro-insights: a systematic review of neuromarketing perspectives across consumer buying stages." Frontiers in Neuroergonomics, 6:1542847. DOI: 10.3389/fnrgo.2025.1542847. CC BY.

### Study Design
- PRISMA systematic review of 109 studies (2020-2025)
- Databases: Scopus, Web of Science, EBSCOhost
- Quality screening: CABS Grades 3/4/4*, ABDC A/A*, Scimago Q1
- First review to examine actual behavior (not proxy or self-reported intent) across all consumer decision stages

### Standardized Definition
"An interdisciplinary area which applies neuroscience and cognitive neuroscience to business. It is about creating brain-friendly content or communication which helps to understand how consumers react at non-conscious level in real time, based on brain operating principles and the responses can be measured by various neuro-metric or non-neuro metric techniques."

### 3×3 Typology
- **Dimensions:** Decision-making stages (pre-purchase, purchase, post-purchase) × Attitude components (affective, behavioral, cognitive)
- **30 functional domains** mapped across the grid
- **Research intensity shading:** Light gray (<20 papers), dark gray (20-40), black (>40)
- **Key finding:** Purchase and post-purchase columns are the thinnest — the field's biggest gap

### Cross-Modal Tool Interaction Framework
- 10 tools cataloged with strengths, blind spots, and pairing logic
- **Pairing principle:** valence needs arousal, arousal needs valence, neural needs attention
- **No single tool captures the full picture** — multimodal is the rigorous approach

### Stage-Specific Neural Correlates
- **Pre-purchase:** Ventral striatum (reward anticipation), amygdala (emotional relevance), prefrontal cortex (logical assessment), P300/LPP (cognitive/emotional processing)
- **Purchase:** Under-explored; challenge of ecological validity; portable neuroimaging as emerging approach
- **Post-purchase:** Ventral striatum (reward/pleasure), prefrontal cortex (value assessment), ERN (post-purchase regret), pupillometry (arousal/cognitive load)

### Market Data
- $1.44B (2023) → $3.11B (2032) at 8.9% CAGR (Mordor Intelligence)
- North America leads; Europe = academic research center; Asia-Pacific = fastest growing

## Source 2: iTMunch Neuromarketing Trends 2025

**Citation:** Uttamchandani, G. (2025). "Neuromarketing in 2025: How Brands Read Consumer Minds." iTMunch.

### Key 2025 Developments
1. **AI-driven insights** — real-time biometric analysis; instant campaign optimization
2. **IoT and wearables** — continuous biometric streaming; real-world context studies
3. **Immersive environments** — VR for shopping experience testing
4. **Generative AI applications** — AI-designed creatives optimized for attention/emotional resonance
5. **Brain-computer interfaces** — early experiments without bulky equipment

### Market Projection
- Global neuromarketing market projected to surpass $3 billion by 2027

### Ethical Concerns Identified
- **Privacy** — biometric and neurological data are highly sensitive
- **Consent** — consumers must clearly agree to testing
- **Manipulation vs. influence** — the line between persuasion and exploitation
- **Regulation** — governments drafting policies to prevent misuse

### Real-World Examples
- **Coca-Cola:** Emotional branding (happiness, nostalgia)
- **Netflix:** Biometric trailer testing for excitement optimization
- **Retailers:** Eye-tracking for product placement and shelf layouts
- **Automotive:** Driver biometrics during test drives

### Future Possibilities
- Predictive emotional models (AI forecasts audience response before launch)
- Real-time adaptive marketing (ads change based on live emotional responses)
- Personalized neuromarketing (tailored experiences per biometric profile)
- Cross-industry expansion (healthcare, education, politics)

### B2B Application
- Decision-makers are human, influenced by subconscious factors
- Neuromarketing helps B2B: compelling whitepapers, optimized website UX, emotionally resonant campaigns

### Key Metrics
- Engagement levels during campaigns
- Emotional resonance scores from biometric analysis
- Sales lift after implementing neuromarketing insights
- ROI compared to traditional A/B testing

## A-Tech Adaptation Principles

1. **Behavioral signals over biometrics** — all neural constructs mapped to on-device behavioral proxies
2. **Transparency by default** — all personalization disclosed with opt-out
3. **Welfare over engagement** — optimize for user outcomes, not time-on-platform
4. **Open-source transparency** — marketing logic auditable by community
5. **Regulatory compliance by design** — privacy-first approach compliant with all current and anticipated regulations
