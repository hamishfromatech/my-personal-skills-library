# Neuromarketing Research Landscape 2026 — Evidence Base

## Source 1: Bashar et al. (2026) — Five-Theme LDA Framework

**Title:** "Mapping the consumer mind: A bibliometric-LDA review of neuromarketing's past, present, and future in consumer behavior"
**Authors:** Abu Bashar, Naglaa, et al.
**Publication:** Strategic Business Research, Vol 2(1), 100092, 2026
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.sbr.2026.100092
**Repository:** https://ray.yorksj.ac.uk/id/eprint/14021/

### Method
- Integrated bibliometric-LDA (Latent Dirichlet Allocation) analysis
- 341 publications (2008–2025)
- Summarizes two decades of neuromarketing research within consumer behavior

### Foundational Problem
"A notable discrepancy between stated preferences and actual behavior has compelled marketing researchers and practitioners to seek more objective measurements."

This discrepancy — people say one thing, do another — is the field's founding problem. Neural and physiological measurement entered marketing to access what surveys cannot reach: subconscious and automatic responses.

### The Five Themes

#### Topic 1: Eco-Neural Analytics
- EEG-based methods to uncover subconscious drivers of **sustainable** consumer preferences
- Dominant keywords: eye tracking, EEG, neuromarketing, consumer, decision
- Represents the application of neural measurement to a specific domain (sustainable consumption)
- Temporal role: Past/Present (established methodology applied to a growing domain)

#### Topic 2: The Visual Gaze
- Eye-tracking research on visual attention, gaze patterns, and consumer response to visual stimuli
- Represents the visual-attention strand of neuromarketing
- Connects to Ramsøy's "corner of death" finding (see `neuromarketing-three-layer-discipline`)
- Temporal role: Past/Present (well-established methodology)

#### Topic 3: Cognitive Foundations
- Integrative and review-oriented conceptual work
- Represents the theoretical foundations — the "why" behind the methods
- Connects to the translational layer in the three-layer discipline framework
- Temporal role: Past/Present (the accumulated theoretical base)

#### Topic 4: Neural Intelligence
- Emphasizes AI integration, neuromarketing intelligence systems, conceptual advancement
- Keywords include: Neuromarketing Intelligence Systems, Conceptual
- Strongly emphasizes AI-driven approaches
- Temporal role: **The future** — where the field is heading

#### Topic 5: Behavioral Neuro-Nexus
- Studies that integrate traditional behavioral methods with neural measurement
- Represents the integration of behavioral science and neuroscience
- Temporal role: **The future** — the convergence frontier

### Temporal Arc
- Topics 1–3 (Eco-Neural Analytics, The Visual Gaze, Cognitive Foundations) = The past and present — established methods, measures, and theory
- Topics 4–5 (Neural Intelligence, Behavioral Neuro-Nexus) = The future — AI integration and behavioral-neural convergence

**The shift:** From *measurement* to *integration*. The first two decades measured (neural responses, gaze patterns, cognitive processes). The next phase integrates (AI into intelligence systems, behavioral methods with neural measurement).

## Source 2: Gazi et al. (2026) — Four Research Clusters

**Title:** "Neuromarketing research through the years: A bibliometric review of technological advancements and multisensory studies"
**Authors:** Md Abu Issa Gazi, Md Mustaqim Roshid, Abdul Waaje, Md Bony Yeamin, Rejaul Karim, Mohammad Bin Amin, Abdul Rahman bin S. Senathirajah, Judit Oláh
**Publication:** Telematics and Informatics Reports, Vol 22, 100332, June 2026
**DOI:** 10.1016/j.teler.2026.100332
**Repository:** https://digitalcommons.mtu.edu/michigantech-p2/2490/

### Method
- Bibliometric method reviewing 307 publications (2015–2024)
- Scopus database
- Techniques: keyword co-occurrence, co-citation, cluster analysis, performance analysis, science mapping
- Tools: VOSviewer, Biblioshiny

### Key Findings

#### Growth
Significant increase in the number of neuromarketing studies over the study period.

#### Geographic Concentration
Notable works from Italy, Spain, and the USA — the field has concentrated in these research communities.

#### Technology Integration
"The literature has been utilizing neuroimaging instruments, artificial intelligence, and virtual reality in the quest to comprehend consumer behavior."

Three technology trends documented:
1. **Neuroimaging instruments** — fMRI, EEG becoming more accessible
2. **Artificial intelligence** — AI prediction models, ML on neural data, automated consumer response estimation
3. **Virtual reality** — immersive environments for studying consumer behavior

#### The Four Research Clusters
1. **Consumer Neuroscience** — neural mechanisms of consumer choice and behavior
2. **Marketing Innovation** — applying neuroscience tools to marketing, advertising, product design
3. **Ethical Issues** — moral concerns: manipulation, autonomy, privacy
4. **Decision-Making Processes** — how the brain constructs preferences, values, purchasing decisions

#### Field Characterization (Conclusion)
"The phenomenon of neuromarketing has been dynamic, interdisciplinary, and technologically paced, while also raising moral concerns."

- **Dynamic:** growing volume, not static
- **Interdisciplinary:** consumer behavior + psychology + neuroscience + marketing + computer science + ethics
- **Technologically paced:** technology (AI, VR, neuroimaging) drives the field forward
- **Moral concerns:** ethics is structurally embedded, not peripheral

#### Future Direction
"It points to the increased use of data-driven and immersive marketing solutions, which access the unconscious reactions of customers."

"These implications suggest that neuromarketing has the potential to elevate marketing by engaging in a personalized manner, while also conveying a message about responsible methods and ethical guidelines as the discipline expands in breadth and depth."

- Personalization is the destination
- Responsible methods and ethical guidelines are the guardrails
- The field is expanding in both breadth and depth

## Cross-Reference Synthesis

### How the Two Reviews Complement Each Other

| Dimension | Bashar (341 pubs) | Gazi (307 pubs) |
|-----------|-------------------|-----------------|
| Analytical method | Bibliometric-LDA | Bibliometric (co-citation, cluster) |
| Organizational unit | 5 LDA themes | 4 research clusters |
| Temporal structure | Past/Present (1-3) → Future (4-5) | 2015-2024 trend |
| Ethics treatment | Implicit in theme structure | Explicit 4th cluster |
| Technology focus | AI as future (Theme 4) | AI/VR/neuroimaging as current trend |
| Founding problem | Stated vs. actual behavior | Dynamic, interdisciplinary, technologically paced |
| Key contribution | Thematic structure | Field character + ethics as structural |

### The Combined Picture
The field:
1. Studies five themes (Bashar), organized into measurement (past) and integration (future)
2. Is organized around four clusters (Gazi), with ethics as a co-equal pillar
3. Was founded on the stated-vs-actual behavior discrepancy (Bashar)
4. Is dynamic, interdisciplinary, technologically paced, and morally concerned (Gazi)
5. Is integrating AI, VR, and neuroimaging (Gazi) — with AI integration as the explicit future (Bashar Theme 4)
6. Is heading toward personalized, data-driven, immersive marketing (Gazi) that must be governed by responsible methods and ethical guidelines (Gazi)

### Connection to the Three-Layer Discipline
- The five themes map to the three layers:
  - Theme 1 (Eco-Neural Analytics) and Theme 2 (The Visual Gaze) = Applied research (Layer 3) — using tools to answer specific questions
  - Theme 3 (Cognitive Foundations) = Translational research (Layer 2) — converting findings into principles
  - Theme 4 (Neural Intelligence) and Theme 5 (Behavioral Neuro-Nexus) = The integration frontier, spanning translational and applied
- The four clusters map to the three layers:
  - Consumer Neuroscience (Cluster 1) and Decision-Making Processes (Cluster 4) = Basic research (Layer 1)
  - Marketing Innovation (Cluster 2) = Applied research (Layer 3)
  - Ethical Issues (Cluster 3) = Cross-cutting — applies to all three layers

### The Ethical Imperative
Both reviews confirm:
- Ethics is a structural feature (Gazi: 4th cluster, co-equal pillar)
- The future themes raise the stakes (Bashar: AI integration amplifies both capability and risk)
- The field itself calls for "responsible methods and ethical guidelines as the discipline expands" (Gazi)

This validates A-Tech's privacy-first approach as aligned with the field's own identified imperative — not an external imposition but a response to what the field itself recognizes.
