# Relevance Economy Evidence Base

## Primary Sources

### 1. The Attention Economy Is Dead. Long Live the Relevance Economy
- **Author:** Prof. Dr. Andreas Fuchs
- **Publication:** Future of Marketing (Substack), June 4, 2026
- **Follow-up to:** "Google I/O 2026 Explained: The Search Bar Is Dead. Long Live the Search Agent"
- **Core thesis:** Reach equals influence is breaking down as AI summaries and AI agents step between brands and buyers. Relevance is the new currency.

### 2. It Is Not Who Ranks That Wins. It Is Who Becomes Relevant
- **Author:** Marco Micheli, Founder, Sequel Consulting
- **Publication:** Sequel Consulting Insights, May 16, 2026
- **Core thesis:** Visibility is now distributed across ecosystems. Competitive advantage comes from being recognized as a trusted source wherever people and machines look for information, not from SERP ranking alone.

## The Data: Attention Economy Breakdown

### Ad Attention Collapse
- 85% of digital ads receive fewer than 2.5 seconds of attention — the minimum needed to create a memorable impression
- Source: Amplified Intelligence, Dr. Karen Nelson-Field
- Gen Z loses active ad attention after 1.3 seconds
- Share of consumers who say social media ads capture their attention dropped from 43% to 31% in a few years
- Source: Kantar Media Reactions, 2024

### AI Summary Intermediation
- When Google shows an AI summary at the top of a search result page, click-through rate for organic results drops by **61%**
- For paid ads: **68%** fewer clicks on the same queries
- Source: Seer Interactive, analysis of 3,119 queries across 25 million impressions, November 2025
- Only 8% of users click a traditional search result when an AI summary is present, vs. 15% without one
- 26% end their browsing session entirely after reading the AI answer
- Source: Pew Research Center, July 2025, based on 68,879 Google searches

### AI Agent Intermediation
- 45% of consumers now use AI during their buying journeys — to research products, interpret reviews, compare options
- Source: IBM Institute for Business Value and National Retail Federation, 2026, survey of 18,000+ consumers across 23 countries
- Gartner forecasts that by 2028, **90% of B2B buying will be intermediated by AI agents**, channeling more than $15 trillion in spending through automated exchanges
- Source: Gartner, 2025
- Visa's global CMO Frank Cooper III framed this shift in April 2026 as the rise of "Business-to-AI" (B2A) — AI agents becoming a new customer segment brands must learn to sell to

## The Data: AI Citation Patterns

### Third-Party Credibility Dominance
- 82–89% of AI citations come from earned media, press coverage, expert mentions, independent reviews — rather than from brand-owned channels
- When paid placements are excluded entirely, the share of non-paid sources rises to ~94%
- Source: Muck Rack, analysis of more than one million AI citations, 2025
- Implication: A brand blog that no one references is close to invisible to an AI agent

### Structured Data Advantage
- Websites that use clear headings, structured data, and FAQ formats are cited far more often in AI answers than those that don't
- Statistics, tables, and answer-first formatting measurably improve citation rates
- Source: BrightEdge AI Search Study, 2025; corroborated by multiple 2026 citation analyses

## The Data: Share of Model Early Signals

### Citation → Conversion Lift
- Brands cited in AI Overviews earn 35% more organic clicks and 91% more paid clicks on the same queries than brands not cited
- Source: Seer Interactive, 2025

### AI-Referred Conversion Advantage
- One benchmark of 312 technology firms found AI visitors converting at 14.2% on average vs. 2.8% for Google organic — roughly a 5x advantage
- Source: Opollo 2026 AI Search Benchmark Report
- Magnitude varies widely: from under 2x in some e-commerce contexts to over 20x in research-heavy B2B
- Most datasets skew toward tech audiences — treat exact multiples with caution
- Direction is consistent across every major study: AI-referred visitors arrive further along the buying journey and convert more readily

### Case Studies
- **Vercel** (developer platform): grew ChatGPT from less than 1% to 10% of all new signups in six months by consistently being recommended in AI answers about its category
  - Source: Vercel CEO Guillermo Rauch, 2025
- **Tally** (8-person SaaS team): made ChatGPT its number-one referral source, with 2,000+ new users per week arriving from AI platforms, without increasing ad spend

## AI Search Growth Context

- AI search platform traffic grew 42.8% year over year in Q1 2026 vs. 2.4% for Google
- AI platforms collectively surpassed 27 billion global visits
- Source: SimilarWeb and Wix Studio "AI Search vs Google" report, Q1 2026
- Search no longer always starts with Google, especially among younger generations who begin on social platforms or AI tools to understand a topic, compare products, or form an initial opinion
- Source: Sequel Consulting, 2026

## Broader AI Adoption Context

- Stanford AI Index Report 2026: global organizational AI adoption has reached 88%
- Generative AI has surpassed 53% adoption across the population in just three years — at a speed greater than the diffusion of the internet and personal computers
- AI is no longer an experimental technology — it is infrastructure entering the economy, content production, education, software, and information distribution pervasively

## Methodological Caveats

1. Most AI citation and conversion benchmarks skew toward tech/B2B audiences — exact multiples should not be extrapolated to consumer or non-tech verticals without validation.
2. AI search platforms are evolving rapidly; citation patterns and conversion rates will shift as models, interfaces, and user behaviors change.
3. "Share of Model" is an emerging metric without a standardized measurement protocol — the dashboard specification in this skill is a proposed framework, not an industry standard.
4. Google remains central for commercial, local, and transactional searches — the Relevance Economy is additive to SEO, not a replacement. The framing "SEO vs. GEO" is limiting; the deeper issue is becoming trustworthy in a distributed environment.
