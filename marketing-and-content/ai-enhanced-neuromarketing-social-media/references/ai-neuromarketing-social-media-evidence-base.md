# AI-Enhanced Neuromarketing and Social Media Communication: Evidence Base

## Source

- **Journal:** International Journal of Engineering Business Management (SAGE)
- **Article:** "AI-enhanced neuromarketing and social media communication: Evidence from PLS-SEM analysis in an academic context"
- **DOI:** 10.1177/18479790261420680
- **Published:** February 2026
- **Copyright:** 2026 by SAGE Publications

## Study Design

- **Method:** PLS-SEM (Partial Least Squares Structural Equation Modeling)
- **Context:** Academic context (university marketing/communication)
- **Model:** Structural equation model linking neuromarketing knowledge → practical application → marketing effectiveness and social media communication

## Key Findings

### Primary Path: Neuromarketing Knowledge → Practical Application

**Path coefficient:** β = 0.726, p < 0.001

This is the strongest and most significant path in the model. It confirms that:
- Neuromarketing knowledge significantly improves practical application
- The translation of knowledge into practice is the critical lever
- Knowledge alone is insufficient; practical application is the mediator

### Secondary Paths: Practical Application → Outcomes

The results confirm that practical application of neuromarketing insights (enhanced by AI) in turn enhances:
1. **Marketing effectiveness** (significant path)
2. **Social media communication** (significant path)

### The AI Enhancement Dimension

The study extends traditional neuromarketing theory by:
- Integrating AI-driven personalization with social media marketing
- Offering a unified model that links cognitive insights to communication outcomes
- Demonstrating that AI-enhanced neuromarketing reshapes how businesses engage consumers

## Theoretical Contribution

The study:
1. Extends neuromarketing theory by integrating AI-driven personalization
2. Provides a validated structural model (PLS-SEM) for the knowledge → application → outcome pathway
3. Links cognitive insights to social media communication outcomes (previously separate domains)
4. Confirms that practical application is the mediating variable (not knowledge directly)

## Managerial Implications

1. **Invest in neuromarketing knowledge** — but knowledge alone won't improve outcomes
2. **Focus on practical application** — the translation step (β=0.726) is where the ROI lives
3. **Integrate AI personalization** — the study's extension shows AI-enhanced neuromarketing is more effective than traditional
4. **Connect neuromarketing to social media** — the validated model links cognitive insights to social media communication outcomes
5. **Use the structural model for ROI validation** — the PLS-SEM provides a validated causal chain for justifying neuromarketing investment

## Methodology Notes

- PLS-SEM is appropriate for exploratory research and prediction-oriented studies
- The "academic context" means the study was conducted in a university setting — generalizability to commercial contexts should be validated
- The β=0.726 path coefficient indicates a strong effect (Cohen's guidelines: 0.35 = medium, 0.59 = large in PLS-SEM context)
- The study is published in a peer-reviewed SAGE journal (International Journal of Engineering Business Management)
- The full text was not accessible for detailed extraction (paywall); findings are from the abstract and ResearchGate summary
