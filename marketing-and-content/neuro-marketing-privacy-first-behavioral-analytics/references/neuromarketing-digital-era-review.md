# Neuromarketing in the Digital Era: Systematic Review

## Source
Journal of Marketing & Social Research, Vol 3 Issue 1 (2026)
Dr. Samir Sarkar (Gauhati University) & Dr. Neetu Sharma (St. Xavier's College, Jaipur)
DOI: https://doi.org/10.61336/jmsr/26-01-19

## Key Findings

### The 90% Subconscious Thesis
Over 90% of consumer decision-making is impacted by subconscious processes that consumers themselves cannot fully express. This validates the shift from survey-based research to behavioral and neural measurement.

### Brain Regions in Consumer Choice
- **Prefrontal cortex (dlPFC):** Cost-benefit evaluation, reflective thinking
- **Ventromedial prefrontal cortex (vmPFC):** Subjective value and emotional integration — frequent vmPFC activity predicts product selection
- **Amygdala:** Emotional salience, fear, excitement, surprise — increases attention and memory encoding
- **Hippocampus:** Memory formation and retrieval, including brand-related memories
- **Nucleus accumbens (NAcc):** Reward anticipation, motivation, pleasure — activation predicts purchase likelihood even when consumers are verbally uncertain
- **Insula:** Disgust, trust, empathy — product packaging that triggers negative insula response creates avoidance

### Decision Fatigue Neural Signature
When consumers face continuous choices, prefrontal cortex executive control degrades. Behavioral signatures include:
- Reliance on default options
- Faster click-to-decision times
- Higher abandonment at complex steps

### Trust Neuroscience
Trustworthy digital interfaces activate medial prefrontal cortex and reduce insula activation. Behavioral proxies:
- Low bounce rate on pricing pages (price transparency reduces neural "pain of paying")
- High completion on privacy disclosure modals
- Positive sentiment on customer service

### Ethical Limitations
- fMRI is costly, slow, and laboratory-bound — limits ecological validity
- EEG cannot localize deep-brain reward structures
- GSR measures arousal but not valence (positive vs. negative)
- Cultural variability means neural responses do not universalize
- Overinterpretation of brain data creates "neuro-hype"

### Future Directions
- AI + neuroscientific measurement for real-time prediction
- VR/AR immersive environments for richer emotional measurement
- Wearable neurotechnology (EEG headbands, biometric smartwatches, emotion-recognition cameras)
- Cultural neuroscience for cross-cultural marketing
- Public policy applications (healthcare, education, sustainability)

## Privacy-First Translation
This review provides the scientific foundation for privacy-first behavioral analytics. The same brain regions activated by traditional neuromarketing stimuli produce observable behavioral signatures that can be measured without biometric capture:
- NAcc reward anticipation → click-through rate, dwell time, return visits
- Amygdala emotional engagement → scroll velocity changes, share/like ratios
- vmPFC value integration → conversion rate, AOV changes
- Insula threat detection → bounce rate, support ticket sentiment
