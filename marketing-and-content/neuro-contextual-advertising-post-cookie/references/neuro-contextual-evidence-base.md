### Measurement Methodology

- **Cross Brain Correlation (CBC)**: Measures how similarly people's brains respond to the same content. High neuro-similarity predicts both memory retention and downstream performance for media campaigns.
- **Neural engagement**: In neuroscience, a measure of how actively viewers' brains respond to a stimulus. High neural engagement indicates the audience is paying attention, emotionally connecting with the message, and forming lasting impressions.
- **Neuro-similarity**: Raised by neuro-contextual campaigns (ads placed in environments closely aligned with viewer's specific interests, emotions, and intentions, not just general topic).

## The Privacy-First Architecture (Why It Works Without Personal Data)

### The Core Insight

"Neuroscience doesn't require knowing who a person is. It measures how the brain responds, not whose brain it is, making it inherently privacy-first."

This is the foundational principle: neural engagement is a universal brain response, not a personal identifier. Two people's brains respond similarly to emotionally congruent content regardless of their demographic profiles.

### The Convergence Thesis

"Neuroscience gives brands a predictive and privacy-first way to understand how ads will resonate at scale. It can provide rich insights into campaign performance even before media spend begins. By measuring universal brain responses, it explains why an ad performs — such as emotional engagement or cognitive ease — not just whether it performs."

### The Planning Layer Thesis

"Context alone is no longer enough — the next evolution is Neuro-Contextual, aligning brand messages with consumers' interests, emotions, and intentions. Ads that feel native to their emotional environment are processed faster, trusted more, and remembered longer."

"I see Neuro-Contextual becoming a core planning layer for every campaign, providing deeper insights into audiences, competitors, and optimization. Emotional context is emerging as the most scalable, predictive, and brand-safe signal in advertising."

— Brian Gleason, CEO Seedtag

## A-Tech Behavioral-Signal Adaptation

### The Principle

Neuro-contextual advertising as practiced by Seedtag uses cloud-based AI to analyze 60M+ URLs daily. A-Tech's adaptation brings the emotional-context extraction on-device, preserving the privacy-first promise at the architecture level (not just the data-collection level).

### The On-Device Emotional-Context Vector

Instead of sending content to a cloud API:

1. **Local NLP extraction**: On-device language model (small, privacy-preserving) analyzes content for:
   - Valence (positive/negative/mixed/neutral)
   - Arousal (high-energy/low-energy)
   - Intent (learn/decide/entertain/compare/purchase)
   - Motivational driver (achievement/affiliation/power/autonomy/curiosity)

2. **Vector generation**: Produces a small (e.g., 128-dimension) emotional-context vector representing the content's emotional landscape

3. **Local matching**: Content/ad ranking computed locally by cosine similarity between user's inferred context state and content emotional-context vectors

4. **Zero data exfiltration**: Content never leaves the device; only the ranking result is displayed

### Behavioral-Signal Neural Engagement Proxies

| Neural Metric | On-Device Behavioral Proxy |
|---|---|
| Attention | Scroll velocity (slow = high attention), time-on-content, interaction depth |
| Emotional connection | Interaction patterns (replay, bookmark, share), sentiment of comments |
| Memory encoding | Delayed recall quiz performance, unaided mention rate, search lift |
| Cognitive ease | Time-to-comprehension, friction-free completion, replay frequency |

These proxies correlate with neural engagement measures but require zero biometric data.
