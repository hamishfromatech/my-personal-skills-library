# Operational Transparency Case Studies

## Financial Services: BBVA ATMs
BBVA redesigned its ATM interfaces so that customers making withdrawals can see a visual representation of currency being counted and organized as the machine performs each task. The visible labor transforms a routine transaction into a perception of care and thoroughness.

## Postal Service: USPS Customer Terminals
The United States Postal Service experimented with customer-facing terminals showing the steps being completed by postal employees at each stage of a transaction. This operational transparency increased customer satisfaction by making hidden labor visible.

## Travel: Kayak/Orbitz Model
Online travel agencies naturally exhibit operational transparency by showing which airlines are being searched while users wait. This visible multi-source search effort increases perceived thoroughness and value compared to opaque instant results.

## Open Source: GitHub Contribution Graphs
GitHub's public contribution graphs are a form of operational transparency for open-source labor. They make individual effort visible to the community, triggering reciprocity (stars, sponsorships) and building reputational value.

## AI/ML: Local Model Execution Interfaces
Emerging local AI tools (e.g., Ollama, LM Studio) show model loading, quantization, and inference progress. This visibility converts "slow local execution" from a drawback into a trust-building feature by proving the code/data stays local.
