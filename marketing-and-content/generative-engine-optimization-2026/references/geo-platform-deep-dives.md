# GEO Platform Deep Dives

## ChatGPT
- Largest market share (~70% of AI search usage as of early 2026)
- Draws from mix of live web search and training data
- Favors comprehensive, well-sourced content with clear expertise signals
- Uses Bing's search index for live retrieval
- Increasingly driving measurable referral traffic through citations
- Conversion rate from referrals: 15.9%

**Optimization:**
- Prioritize entity authority signals
- Comprehensive coverage with expert quotes
- Strong backlink profiles increase Common Crawl exposure

## Perplexity
- Heavily citation-focused; uses real-time web search
- Strong preference for recent, up-to-date content
- More transparent about sources than other platforms
- Highest conversion rates for SaaS among AI platforms
- Uses vector indexing (different from Bing/Google)
- Conversion rate from referrals: 10.5%

**Optimization:**
- Content freshness matters most here
- Update cornerstone content at least monthly
- Clear sourcing and methodology signals

## Google AI Overviews / Gemini
- Integrate traditional search ranking signals with AI synthesis
- Content ranking well in organic search tends to perform well in AI Overviews
- Schema markup and structured data may influence selection
- Local relevance matters for location-based queries
- Conversion rate from referrals: ~5%

**Optimization:**
- Schema signals matter most for this platform
- Strong Google SEO still translates to Gemini visibility
- Implement FAQ schema and comparison tables aggressively

## Claude
- Tends to synthesize rather than quote directly
- Favors well-structured, logical content
- Apple announced integration into Safari (significant future influence)
- Conversion rate from referrals: 5%

**Optimization:**
- Logical argument structure
- Well-organized, hierarchical content
- Entity clarity and disambiguation

## Critical Platform Differences
- Only 11% domain overlap between ChatGPT and Perplexity citation sources
- Same brand can see citation volumes differ by 615x between Grok and Claude
- Success on one platform does not guarantee visibility on another
- Each platform uses different indexing and retrieval technology
