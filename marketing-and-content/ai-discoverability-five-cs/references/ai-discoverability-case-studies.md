# AI Discoverability Case Studies

## Case Study 1: Sarah Stahl & Lake.com
**Strategy:** Micro-creators with real proximity to real audiences.
**Result:** $2M revenue on a $30K budget.
**AI Discoverability Lesson:** Trust compounds faster than ad spend. Micro-creators are conversational nodes that AI systems weight heavily.

## Case Study 2: Cisco
**Strategy:** 10,000+ employees trained and certified to speak on behalf of the brand.
**Framework:** Distributed authorship. Trust Portfolio.
**AI Discoverability Lesson:** When 10,000 voices are talking about your brand in specific, credible ways, AI agents surface that brand more frequently and accurately.

## Case Study 3: Supabase
**Strategy:** Radical transparency and building in public.
**Result:** 70,000+ GitHub stars, 200,000+ Discord members, $500M+ valuation in 3 years.
**AI Discoverability Lesson:** Open-source everything + consistent current content = AI systems treat you as an authority.

## Case Study 4: Vercel & Next.js
**Strategy:** Framework-first, platform-second. Invest in the open ecosystem before monetizing.
**Result:** Next.js became the default React framework. Vercel grew to $2.5B+ valuation.
**AI Discoverability Lesson:** When your open-source project dominates conversations, AI agents recommend your paid platform naturally.

## Case Study 5: Tailwind CSS
**Strategy:** Engaged with criticism constructively. Controversy became fuel for community growth.
**Result:** 85,000+ GitHub stars. Adopted by major companies globally.
**AI Discoverability Lesson:** Conversation (even critical conversation) improves AI discoverability more than silence.
