# Tether TranslatePsy: Offline Open AI from the Stablecoin Camp

## Overview
Tether Data released three open-source, offline-runnable translation models targeting underserved language communities — the sharpest current example of an unexpected actor (a stablecoin issuer) executing the privacy-first, efficiency-first open-AI playbook: tiny parameters, local execution, no cloud dependency.

## When to Use
- Surveying the local-AI/on-device model landscape beyond the usual lab names
- Evaluating small-model efficiency claims (params vs benchmark vs storage)
- Assessing strategic diversification by crypto-native companies into open AI
- Planning multilingual local deployment (healthcare/education/humanitarian offline contexts)

## Core Process / Workflow
1. **Inventory the family.** TranslatePsy-AfriSLM (19 African languages — Swahili, Hausa, Zulu — covering regions near half of Africa's population), TranslatePsy-AfriNano, TranslatePsy-EuroNano (9 European languages, 90 translation directions).
2. **Check the efficiency claims.** Smallest AfriSLM (800M params) reportedly outperformed Meta's NLLB-3.3B on FLORES-200; EuroNano needs just 36MB of storage (94% less than comparable offline solutions), English used as pivot.
3. **Verify deployment characteristics.** Runs directly on smartphones/laptops with no internet; full-precision + quantized versions on Hugging Face; research to be presented at EMNLP 2026.
4. **Read the strategic context.** Builds on the 2025 QVAC platform for localized AI; Tether Data has pushed user-operated, peer-to-peer models since 2024; the solar-powered kiosk network across Africa is framed as a distribution surface for localized AI content. Ardoino: "Language should not determine who can benefit from artificial intelligence."
5. **Apply the discipline.** For any adoption: benchmark locally on your language pairs; treat launch-time availability as promotion, not pricing; note the model family's independence from the stablecoin's core product.

## A-Tech Alignment
- **Open source:** fully open weights with quantized builds — the distribution pattern the library favors.
- **Privacy:** offline-by-design; zero cloud egress for translation workloads.
- **Financial freedom:** 36MB-class models make multilingual AI feasible on commodity hardware — near-zero marginal cost for underserved-market products.
- **Practical:** a template for "efficiency-first open AI" claims you can audit (params/storage/benchmark triad).

## Honesty Caveats
