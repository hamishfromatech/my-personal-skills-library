## Industry-Specific Preparedness

### Healthcare
- HIPAA minimum-necessary standard effectively requires purpose limitation
- 63% gap = immediate compliance exposure
- Joint advisory adds federal naming to existing HIPAA obligation

### Financial Services
- SOX audit trails must now include agent decisions
- Model version anchoring required for model risk management
- Kill switch tested as part of operational resilience frameworks

### Technology / SaaS
- Broad Copilot deployments create shadow agent risk
- Internal copilots, embedded SaaS agents, departmental pilots = unmapped attack surface
- First step: complete inventory before any other control
