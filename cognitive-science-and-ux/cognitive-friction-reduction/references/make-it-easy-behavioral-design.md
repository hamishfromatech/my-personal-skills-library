# Reference: Make It Easy — Behavioral Design Made Simpler
**Source:** https://www.makeit.tools/strategies/make-it-easy
**Date:** Ongoing
**Key Points:**

- Behavioral science approach to design: be intentional about how friction is overcome or leveraged
- Ease of action directly predicts likelihood of action
- Intentional friction: sometimes friction is desirable (e.g., confirmation for destructive actions)
- Make it Easy as core behavioral design principle
