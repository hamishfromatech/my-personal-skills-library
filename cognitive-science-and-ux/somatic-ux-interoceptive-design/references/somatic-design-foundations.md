# Somatic Design Foundations

## Sources and Research Base

### Primary Source
**Yuki Kobayashi, "Good UX Informs. Great UX Awakens. The Rise of Somatic Design." Medium / Design Bootcamp, March 2, 2026.**
- Core thesis: AI products are accurate but exhausting because they ignore the body
- Personal narrative: author experienced nervous system dysregulation from chronic overstimulation; doctors found no medical issues; Somatic Experiencing therapy revealed mind-body disconnect
- Key insight: "Your mind has decided you're safe. Your body hasn't gotten the message yet."

### KTH Royal Institute of Technology — Soma Design
**Professor Kristina Höök and colleagues**
- Methodology: Design begins with the designer's own body, not third-person user research
- Core move: cultivate interoceptive awareness (internal sensory signals: stomach tightening, shallow breath, raised shoulders)
- First-person somatic awareness as a design instrument
- **Breathing Light** case study: ambient installation pulsing softly in sync with user's breath; goal is not to improve breathing but to create an opportunity to notice breath
- Design question shifts from "Can the user understand this interface?" to "What is happening in the user's body during this interaction?"

### Felt Ethics Framework (Höök research)
- New category of ethical failure: something can be technically compliant, legally permissible, and privacy-sound — and still feel wrong in the body
- Bodily discomfort is a legitimate source of ethical knowledge
- Cyclical process: sharpen somatic awareness → receive bodily discomfort as ethical signal → iterate design
- Example: breathing-guidance device overriding natural rhythm to achieve "calmer" data reading improves numbers while user feels controlled or invaded

### The Somatic Turn
- Cross-disciplinary movement reshaping philosophy, cognitive science, and design
- Central argument: we cannot understand human experience if we only study the brain
- We are embodied beings; thinking, feeling, and decision-making are inseparable from physical state
- As generative AI simulates more cognitive functions, the value of what cannot be digitized grows
- The felt sense of being alive in a body is what AI cannot replicate

## Three Interconnected Concepts — Architecture

```
Somatic Turn          (philosophical current)
    └── Soma Design   (concrete design methodology)
            └── Felt Ethics (ethical framework grounded in bodily experience)
```

## Key Quotes for A-Tech Positioning

> "Every product asks something of its users. Most products ask: 'Can you receive what we've prepared for you?' Somatic UX asks a different question: 'Does our product help you notice what's already happening inside you?' One delivers an answer. The other opens a door."

> "The products that will earn lasting trust — in the age of AI — are the ones that make users feel more like themselves, not less."
