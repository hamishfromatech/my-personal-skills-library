# Scaffolded Cognitive Friction — Research Sources

## Primary Source

**Kuangzhe Xu, Yu Shen, Longjie Yan, Yinhui Ren. "Cognitive Agency Surrender: Defending Epistemic Sovereignty via Scaffolded AI Friction." arXiv:2603.21735v1 [cs.HC], March 23, 2026.**

### Study Design
- **Corpus:** 1,223 high-confidence AI-HCI papers from 2023 through March 9, 2026
- **Method:** Zero-shot semantic classification using BART-large-MNLI with ultra-conservative confidence threshold (τ=0.7)
- **Baseline:** ~8,000 papers from OpenAlex API using ontological intersection of Human-Computer Interaction (C107457646) AND Artificial Intelligence (C154945302)
- **Retention rate:** 15.3% of initial corpus passed the confidence threshold

### Key Findings
| Paradigm | 2023-2024 | 2025 | Early 2026 |
|----------|-----------|------|------------|
| Frictionless (efficiency, reduced effort) | ~68% | ~66% | ~67.3% |
| Machine Autonomy (AI agents, reflection) | ~12% | ~14% | **19.6%** |
| Human Epistemic Sovereignty (critical thinking, preventing automation bias) | ~18% | **19.1%** | **13.1%** |

- The 2025 peak in sovereignty defense was "abruptly suppressed" in early 2026 by the surge in machine autonomy optimization
- This structural divergence quantifies the escalating marginalization of human epistemic agency

### Theoretical Frameworks Referenced
- **Clark & Chalmers (1998):** Extended Mind thesis
- **Chiriatti et al. (2024, 2025):** System 0 — non-biological cognitive extension layer augmenting System 1 (intuition) and System 2 (analytical reasoning)
- **Stanovich (2018):** Cognitive miserliness — evolutionary tendency to conserve metabolic energy
- **Sweller (1988):** Cognitive Load Theory (intrinsic, extraneous, germane load)
- **Bjork & Bjork (2011):** Desirable difficulties for enhanced learning
- **Kahneman (2011):** Thinking, Fast and Slow (System 1 / System 2)
- **Nemeth (1986):** Minority influence theory — deliberate dissent forces divergent thinking
- **Friston (2010):** Free-energy principle / predictive processing
- **Festinger (1957):** Cognitive dissonance
- **Botvinick et al. (2001):** Conflict monitoring and cognitive control (anterior cingulate cortex)
- **Inzlicht et al. (2018):** The effort paradox — overcoming resistance enhances knowledge valuation
- **Elish (2019):** Moral crumple zones — humans absorbing liability for algorithmic failures
- **Zipf (1949):** Law of Least Effort

### Proposed Interventions
1. **Scaffolded Cognitive Friction:** Repurpose Multi-Agent Systems as cognitive forcing functions (computational Devil's Advocates)
2. **Multimodal Computational Phenotyping:**
   - Gaze Transition Entropy (GTE) for visual sampling vigor
   - Task-Evoked Pupillometry for locus coeruleus-norepinephrine arousal
   - fNIRS for prefrontal cortex activation
3. **Hierarchical Drift Diffusion Models (HDDM):** Mathematical decoupling of starting-point bias (zz) from drift rate (vv)

### Three Core Hypotheses
- **H1:** Scaffolded friction via Devil's Advocate reduces agency surrender, parallel-mediated by PFC activation, elevated GTE, and pupillary dilation
- **H2:** Protective effect follows inverted-U relationship; friction shock occurs when friction exceeds working memory capacity
- **H3:** Scaffolded friction attenuates Illusion of Explanatory Depth more effectively than unidirectional XAI

### Governance Implications
- EU AI Act mandates "substantive human oversight" for high-risk AI systems
- Frictionless interfaces transform legal oversight into a rubber stamp
- Intentionally designed friction is a foundational technical prerequisite for global AI governance

### Domain Implementation Guidelines
- **Mandatory Friction:** Cognitive security, cyber-physical systems, healthcare, judiciary
- **Zero-Friction Mandates:** Low-stakes information retrieval, time-critical domains
