# Skill: Outcome-Based AI Pricing

## Summary
Pricing AI products based on tangible results delivered (work completed, problems solved) rather than seats, tokens, or access. This model aligns revenue with customer value and reflects AI's role as an autonomous teammate, not just a tool.

## Core Principles
- **Value alignment**: Charge for resolved tickets, shipped code, generated documents — not API calls or seats
- **Cost variability acceptance**: Accept inference cost risk in exchange for perfect value alignment with customers
- **Hard ROI positioning**: Avoid "soft ROI" copilot territory where customers question value; deliver measurable, undeniable outcomes
- **Hybrid models**: Base subscription + outcome tiers provide predictability while capturing upside

## Three AI Business Models
1. **Copilots** — AI sidekicks enhancing productivity; priced per seat or consumption (e.g., GitHub Copilot)
2. **Agents** — Autonomous AI executing entire workflows; priced on tangible ROI, workflow completion, or cost savings
3. **AI-Enabled Services** — Blend automation with human oversight; priced per output or market rate for equivalent service

## Charge Metrics
| Metric | Type | Best For |
|--------|------|----------|
| Consumption (tokens/API calls) | Technical | Developers, data scientists |
| Workflow (per completed task) | Practical | Task-oriented buyers |
| Outcome (per successful result) | Value-aligned | Enterprise buyers seeking clear ROI |

## A-Tech Alignment
- **Financial freedom**: Captures maximum value from AI products; customers pay for results
- **Practical implementation**: Real-world playbook from BVP portfolio companies
- **Open-source AI**: Can apply to open-source products by charging for outcomes, not software access

## Applications
- **A-Coder (IDE)**: Price per successful code review, bug fix, or feature shipped — not per seat
- **Be Practical (Book/Playbooks)**: Teach founders how to price AI tools by outcome
- **Open Source AI Builder's Club**: Help members monetize their open-source AI tools via outcome-based SaaS layers

## Implementation Steps
1. Define the unambiguous, measurable outcome your AI delivers
2. Calculate cost variance (best case vs. worst case inference cost)
3. Set outcome price at 10-20% of customer value captured
4. Offer hybrid: base platform fee + outcome credits
5. Build operational discipline to track true unit economics

## Key Metrics
- AI resolution rate / accuracy
- % of work completed autonomously
- Developer acceptance rate (for code tools)
- Time from idea to prototype
- Gross margin target: 50-60% (vs. 80-90% SaaS)
