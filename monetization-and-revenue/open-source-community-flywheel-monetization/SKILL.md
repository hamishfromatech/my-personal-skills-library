# Skill: Open Source Community Flywheel & Monetization
## Category: Community / Revenue / Open Source
## Alignment: A-Tech Values ★★★★★

### Overview
Open-source communities can achieve sustainable funding without compromising openness. The community flywheel model turns contributors into advocates, advocates into customers, and customers into sponsors—while keeping the core open and accessible.

### Key Concepts
- **Open Core**: Open-source foundation + proprietary enterprise extensions
- **Dual Licensing**: GPL/commercial license split
- **Community-Led Growth**: Organic expansion through member advocacy
- **Value Stacking**: Multiple revenue streams (support, hosting, premium features, education)
- **Membership Ladders**: Free → Supporter → Builder → Sponsor tiers
- **Business Source License (BSL)**: Free for most uses, requires license for production SaaS

### A-Tech Alignment
- **Open-Source AI**: Core remains open; monetize convenience and enterprise needs
- **Data Privacy**: Community trust is the asset—never compromise it
- **Financial Freedom**: Multiple revenue streams create resilience
- **Practical Implementation**: Proven models from Redis, MongoDB, GitLab, Supabase

### Applications
#### A-Coder (IDE)
- Open-source core IDE + premium cloud sync, team features, enterprise SSO
- Community plugin marketplace with revenue share for contributors
- "Builder Pro" tier with advanced local AI models and priority support
- Enterprise licensing for air-gapped deployments

#### Be Practical (Playbooks)
- Create "Open Source Monetization Playbook" with real case studies
- Document value-stacking strategy for solo founders
- Teach dual-licensing legal basics for builders
- Build "Community Flywheel" operational template

#### Open Source AI Builder's Club
- Implement membership tiers: Free → Supporter ($10/mo) → Builder ($50/mo) → Sponsor ($500/mo)
- Create community bounty system: members fund features they want
- Host "build in public" challenges with prizes and recognition
- Develop shared revenue from community-built tools
- Use Mighty Networks or similar for paid community layer

### Revenue Streams to Stack
1. Membership/subscription community access
2. Premium tooling and SaaS add-ons
3. Consulting and implementation services
4. Training and certification programs
5. Sponsored content and job board
6. Enterprise licensing and support
7. Merchandise and events

### Implementation Notes
- Free tier must be genuinely useful, not crippled
- Always transparent about funding model
- Share revenue with top contributors
- Governance should reflect community input, not just sponsor interests
- Document the business model openly—nothing to hide
