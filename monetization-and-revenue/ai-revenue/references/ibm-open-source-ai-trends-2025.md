# Reference: Open-source AI in 2025 — IBM
**Source:** https://www.ibm.com/think/news/2025-open-ai-trends
**Date:** 2026
**Key Points:**

- Shift from open models to open AI systems (integrations, verticals, classifiers, parsers)
- 51% of businesses using open-source tools saw positive ROI vs. 41% not using open-source
- Open source = cost-effective + velocity
- EU AI Act driving ethical AI practices; transparency as business opportunity
- IBM Granite 3.1: open model for safe, private enterprise inference
- Models getting smaller and better (Llama 3.3 70B ≈ 3.1 405B performance)
- Multimodal models natively across text, image, speech
- Linux Foundation: 100,000+ developers contributing to 68 hosted projects from 3,000+ organizations
- Collaboration between large enterprises on foundation models becoming more common
