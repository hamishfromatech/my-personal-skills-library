## Market Data
- 43% of SaaS companies now use hybrid pricing (up from 28% in 2024)
- Projected to reach 61% by 2028
- 41% of enterprise SaaS companies were implementing hybrid pricing by late 2023 (now dominant)
- AI-native companies are abandoning seat-based pricing faster than traditional SaaS

## The Spectrum
Hybrid pricing exists on a spectrum, not as a binary:

1. **Platform + Usage Caps** (most common): Flat fee + metered overage
2. **Platform + Outcome** (emerging): Base fee + per-result charge
3. **Platform + Agent Seats** (new): Base fee + per-AI-agent charge
4. **Full Hybrid** (advanced): Base + usage + outcome + agent seats

## Why the Shift?
- Seat-based pricing doesn't capture AI value (agents don't need seats)
- Pure usage pricing creates bill shock and uncertainty
- Pure outcome pricing is hard to measure and disputes arise
- Hybrid balances predictability (base fee) with value alignment (variable)

## Enterprise Preferences
- Enterprises want predictable budgets → base platform fee satisfies this
- Enterprises also want to pay for results → outcome component satisfies this
- Procurement teams resist pure usage due to quarter-end surprises
- Finance teams resist pure outcome due to measurement disputes
