# Citi 53% Milestone: Open-Weight Usage Majority

## Overview
Citi's "Inference Ahead" note (published ~Sept 1 2026) documents the crossing: open-weight models made up **53% of token volume on Vercel's AI Gateway on Aug 25 2026**, up 24 percentage points from late June. Over the same period, the performance gap between the strongest proprietary and strongest open-weight models narrowed from 9 to 3 points. This is the adoption-side milestone that completes the library's Mozilla thesis (33% usage share / 4% revenue share, July 2026) — usage share for open weights has now crossed the majority line on a major routing platform within eight weeks.

## The Evidence Base
- **Citi "Inference Ahead" report** (Sept 1 2026, via Proactive Investors): 53% open-weight token share on Vercel AI Gateway as of Aug 25, +24pp vs late June.
- **Convergence datapoint:** strongest-proprietary vs strongest-open performance gap 9 → 3 points over the same window.
- **Economics datapoint:** DeepSeek reportedly generated ~$71M revenue through July 2026 (~10x its full-year 2025 total) at an 83% gross margin on paid API access — open-weight models supporting attractive economics at the hosted layer.
- **Ecosystem context:** Citi flags the NVIDIA–Hugging Face deal ($12.9B, announced Sept 3) and NVIDIA–Poolside licensing ($6B) as moves that could strengthen the open-weight ecosystem while driving compute demand.
- **Regulatory frame:** Citi notes proprietary frontier models are "held back by regulatory constraints" — an unusual framing that positions openness as the constraint-free path.

## Core Findings (the three readings)
1. **Usage majority arrived in 2026, not eventually.** 24pp in 8 weeks is the fastest adoption shift in the library's records; the open-weight usage share crossed 50% on a production routing platform. Use the dated, specific figure (53%, Aug 25 2026, Vercel AI Gateway) rather than vague "rising" claims.
2. **Capability convergence is the enabler.** The 9→3 point gap narrowing explains the adoption: when open models are within noise of proprietary on the workloads that matter, the routing choice becomes an economics decision, not a capability one.
3. **Usage majority ≠ revenue majority.** This measures TOKENS, not dollars. The revenue question is unchanged from the Mozilla finding (open weights: ~33% usage → 4% revenue as of July 2026) — value still flows to hosting and distribution, not weight creators. The 53% milestone strengthens the "who captures the value" question rather than answering it.

## When to Use
- Briefings or content citing the state of open-weight adoption — this is the most recent dated milestone.
- Calibrating "open source crossed the majority" claims with a specific platform, date, and source.
- Explaining why hosted-inference businesses (Fireworks, Baseten, Vercel, OpenRouter) are the revenue capture point.
- Countering "open models are years behind" assumptions with the 3-point gap figure.

## NOT For
- Claiming open-weight REVENUE share crossed 50% — tokens ≠ dollars.
- Consumer chatbot market share (different denominator, different population).
- Model-quality claims beyond "gap narrowed 9→3 points" — no specific model is endorsed.

## Core Process / Workflow
1. **Quote the milestone precisely.** "53% of token volume on Vercel's AI Gateway (Aug 25 2026), per Citi" — platform, date, metric, source. Never round "more than half" into "dominates."
2. **Pair with the revenue counterweight.** In the same brief, cite the Mozilla 33%-usage/4%-revenue figure so the audience sees usage and revenue diverging — the gap IS the story.
3. **Use the 3-point gap as the convergence evidence.** When someone claims open models are far behind, cite the narrowing trend (9→3 in 8 weeks), not a single snapshot.
4. **Watch the next read.** The milestone is a single-platform datapoint; triangulate with OpenRouter and Mozilla data before extrapolating market-wide. Citi flagged the LEAP event as an early read on enterprise deployment.

## CYCLE-22-RUN-2 UPDATE (2026-09-10): Enterprise-Scale Confirmation

**Source:** Inside AI News (Sept 6, 2026) via OpenRouter data: open-model usage reached **58% of AI workloads, up from 10% a year ago** — corroborating Citi's 53% Vercel-token milestone with an independent enterprise-shift datapoint. Named corporate movers: **AT&T** (open models 20% in May → 40% now; CDO Andy Markus: "We believe it could go much, much higher"; saving up to 80% on AI costs), Airbnb, Deloitte. Atlas Cloud CEO Jerry Tang: Chinese open models (Moonshot, DeepSeek, Alibaba) deliver **80–90% of closed capability at 20% of the price** — "Mazdas do a fine job getting you where you need to go, and Maseratis are simply not worth the investment."

**The strategic frame holds:** usage majority ≠ revenue majority — the Mozilla 33%-usage/4%-revenue counterweight stands, and the enterprise shift SHARPENS the who-captures-value question (revenue flows to hosting and distribution). The Citi 53% (Vercel tokens) and 58% (OpenRouter workloads) figures are now two independent platform confirmations of the same adoption arc.

**Watch item:** whether a major cloud ships a metered open-model product — the Mozilla prediction this milestone leaves open.
