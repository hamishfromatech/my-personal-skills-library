# Open-Source Contribution ROI 2026 — Evidence Base

## Source

**Linux Foundation (February 24, 2026)** — "ROI for Open Source Software Contribution: Insight from the Open Source ROI Survey and Economic Model." Released at the Linux Foundation Member Summit, Napa, California.

**Survey:** 500+ IT leaders, global, conducted by Linux Foundation Research with survey distribution partner ISG.

---

## Headline Findings

### ROI by engagement form

| Engagement form | ROI multiple |
|---|---|
| Code contribution | 3.6x |
| Community contribution | 3.2x |
| Financial contribution | 2.4x |
| Foundation membership | 4.8x |
| **Overall** | **2-5x** |

- Two-thirds of respondents report increased ROI after contributing and expect returns to improve over time.
- Between 2018 and 2025, the top 100 open-source contributors yielded $23.2 billion in benefits from a $3.9 billion investment — a 6x increase.

### The hidden maintenance tax of passive consumption

| Cost | Amount |
|---|---|
| Proprietary avoidance value | $3.5M (what orgs would spend on proprietary tech or internal builds without OSS) |
| Workaround tax | $670K/yr average; 49% of respondents build internal workarounds |
| Private-fork maintenance | 5,160 labor hours / $258K per release cycle |

### Benefits beyond the bottom line

| Benefit | Statistic |
|---|---|
| Talent attraction and retention | 68% of respondents say contribution makes it easier to hire and retain top talent |
| Product development speed | +10% on average |
| Security response | 66% report upstream maintainers respond faster to contributors' issues and bug reports |
| Roadmap influence | 84% of contributors report successfully influencing project roadmaps more than half the time |
