### AI User Behavior (RevenueCat 2026)
- AI apps earn 41% more per payer than non-AI apps
- AI apps churn 30% faster than non-AI apps
- Typical freemium conversion: 2.1% at day 35
- Over 60% of top-grossing apps run multiple revenue streams

### Subscription Price Points (2026)
- Consumer entry: $8-10/mo (ChatGPT Go, Perplexity Standard)
- Consumer premium: $15-25/mo (ChatGPT Plus $20, Claude Pro)
- Prosumer/Pro: $30-200/mo
- Team/business: $25-60/seat/mo

### Ad Revenue Data
- OpenAI Feb 2026 ad launch: $100M annualized in 6 weeks

- ChatGPT consumer subs: $17B+ annualized projected for 2026

### Usage-Based Data
- 77% of large software companies include usage-based pricing (Metronome 2026)
- Usually as hybrid overage on top of subscription

### Ad Economics Levers
- Ad-eligible prompt rate: 15-40%
- Fill rate: 50-90%
- RPM: $3-50+
- CTR: 3-15%
- Revenue share to app: 40-70%

## Case Study Details

### ChatGPT
- Model mix: freemium + ads + subscription + enterprise + licensing
- $25B+ annualized revenue
- $17B+ consumer subs projected 2026
- $2.5B projected ad revenue 2026
- $100M ad revenue in 6 weeks post-launch
- 200M+ weekly active users

### Perplexity
- Model mix: subscription-first + emerging ad formats
- Hundreds of millions annualized from Pro subscription
- New ad formats shipping 2025-2026

### Claude (Anthropic)
- Model mix: subscription + API + enterprise
- No ad layer — deliberately ad-free
- Enterprise contracts up 180% YoY
- Anthropic revenue in single digits of billions

### Midjourney
- Model mix: pure subscription with credit-based usage
- Hundreds of millions in revenue
- Minimal team size
- Credits work when value per credit is legible

### Vertical AI SaaS (Harvey, medical AI)
- Model mix: seat-based subscription + enterprise licensing
- Scaling into tens to hundreds of millions
- B2B doesn't need ads or usage-based — seat + licensing sufficient

### Billing and Subscription

- Stripe Billing (web and native)

### Usage-Based Billing
- Metronome (now part of Stripe) — real-time metering
- Orb — modern usage-based billing
- Flexprice, Vayu, Schematic — newer AI-specific entrants

### Advertising Supply-Side
- Thrad (explicitly AI-publisher-focused)
- Classic mobile ad networks (AdMob, Meta Audience Network) — lack prompt-intent matching
- Retail media networks (Amazon, Walmart Connect)

### Affiliate and Commerce
- Impact, Skimlinks, Amazon Associates
- AI-specific commerce routing (emerging category)

### Enterprise Contract and Licensing
- DocuSign, Ironclad (contract workflow)
- Custom legal (most licensing deals require bespoke contract work)

## The 2028 Outlook (5 Trends)

1. Ad-supported free tiers universal for consumer AI by end 2027
2. Agentic commerce matures into real revenue channel (material revenue by 2027)
3. Outcome-based pricing pilots expand (vertical agents lead)
4. Cross-app AI ad networks consolidate (3-5 material players by 2028)
5. Licensing becomes structural revenue line
