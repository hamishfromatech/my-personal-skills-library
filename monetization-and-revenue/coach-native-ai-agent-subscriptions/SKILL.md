# The Paywalled Expertise Agent: Subscriptions From a Coach's Methodology

## Overview
The third monetization pillar between digital products (asset, slow feedback loop) and B2B services (fast, unscaleable): **encode a specific methodology as a paywalled AI agent** and sell recurring access. The structural case: build cost one-time and dropping (8–20 hours in 2026 vs 20–60 hours of prompt engineering in 2024), margins above 90% after build (the agent handles all messages), platform-portable across web/WhatsApp/Messenger/Instagram DM, and 2–4 hours/month of maintenance — the lowest time-labor-curve of any recurring model.

## The four use cases that convert (specificity is the whole game)
1. **Deliverable rewriter** — rewrite the user's resume/sales script/landing copy in the coach's methodology. Highest-converting category. Ship this first.
2. **Diagnostic intake** — structured assessment returning a personalized plan (fitness, financial, business).
3. **Decision walker** — step-through guidance for a recurring hard decision (negotiation, hard conversation, parenting scenario).
4. **Between-session continuity** — for existing 1:1 clients, handles the question stream between sessions; retention tool + add-on revenue line.

"An agent that knows everything I teach" is the low-converting mistake; buyers can't ask what they don't know exists.

## The economics that matter
**Benchmarks by audience size** (assume $29–$49/mo, 1–3% take rate, 75–88% monthly retention):

| Email list | 90 days | 12 months | 24-month cap |
|---|---|---|---|
| 500–1,000 | $200–$600 | $800–$1,800 | $2.5K–$4K |
| 1,000–5,000 | $1,000–$2,500 | $3,000–$6,500 | $8K–$15K |
| 5,000–20,000 | $3,500–$8,000 | $10,000–$22,000 | $25K–$50K |
| 20,000+ | $8,000–$25,000 | $30,000–$75K | $80K–$200K |

**The $5K/month milestone** = a 3,000–6,000-person list at month 9–12. Case: Sarah, business-writing coach, 4,800-subscriber list — "deliverable rewriter" agent, 14-hour build, $29/mo with 4-message preview: 31 subscribers week 1 (0.65%), $2,523 MRR month 3, **$5,162 MRR month 9** at 84% retention, 3 hrs/month effort.

## The pricing structure that converts
- **Entry tier $19–$47/mo** for rewriters/intakes (volume tier); **premium $77–$197/mo** for decision walkers/continuity (high-use tier); **annual at 25–35% discount**.
- **The 3–5 message free preview before the paywall is the single highest-converting structural element** (12–28% paywall conversion after preview). The buyer experiences the agent before committing.
- Launch to the owned audience first — no paid ads in month one; expect 0.5–2% of the list in week 1.

## Maintenance is 2–4 hours/month, allocated to three levers
1. **Retention monitoring** (1 hr): review cancellation reasons — most common is "not using it enough"; proactive monthly check-ins lift retention 5–10pp.
2. **Knowledge-base updates** (1–2 hrs): stale knowledge bases are the biggest month-4–6 churn predictor. Spend the 4–6 hours curating it up front; that hour is worth ~10 hours of prompt tuning.
3. **Funnel tuning** (0.5–1 hr): most coaches leave 10–25% of preview-conversion potential unadjusted.

## Honest limitations
- **Audience floor:** under 500 subscribers, plan 6–12 months to reach $500 MRR — the agent works, the audience math takes longer.
- **Niche specificity beats audience size:** 1,500-person specialist list converts dramatically better than a 5,000 generalist one.
- **Knowledge-base quality is the churn predictor** — raw unfiltered uploads produce inconsistent answers and month-4–6 cancellations.
- Platform-vendor framing: the benchmarks come from a platform's own deployment data — treat as upper-middle-of-range directional, not audited.
