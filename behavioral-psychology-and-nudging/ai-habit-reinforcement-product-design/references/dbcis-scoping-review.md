# AI-Powered Digital Behavior Change Interventions (DBCIs) Scoping Review

## Scope
Reviewed 23 AI-powered Digital Behavior Change Interventions targeting real-world health behaviors (physical activity, diet, medication adherence, etc.).

## Key Findings
- AI-enhanced DBCIs show strong potential for personalized, context-specific behavior change support
- Most effective techniques: **self-monitoring, goal setting, prompts/cues**
- Technology is still evolving; complex data handling and tailored support are improving but not yet mature
- Effective AI habit systems combine: behavioral science foundation + ML personalization + real-time feedback

## Transfer to Non-Health Domains
The same three-component structure applies to developer tools, learning platforms, and community engagement:
1. **Behavioral science foundation:** Habit loop, reward timing, cognitive load management
2. **ML personalization:** Predict lapse, adapt difficulty, tailor messaging
3. **Real-time feedback:** Context-aware nudges, immediate reinforcement, progress visualization
