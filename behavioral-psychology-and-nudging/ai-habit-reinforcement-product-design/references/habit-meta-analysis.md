# Habit Formation Meta-Analysis

## Key Statistics
- **Median formation time:** 66 days (range: 18–254 days)
- **Meta-analysis effect size:** Standardized mean difference = 0.69 (95% CI: 0.49–0.88) for habit score improvements after interventions
- **Individual variation:** Wide range reflects behavior complexity, baseline automaticity, personality traits, and environmental stability

## Most Effective Behavior Change Techniques (BCTs)
Across 41 studies, the most commonly used and effective BCTs:
1. **Feedback and monitoring** — Real-time tracking with visible progress
2. **Goal setting** — Specific, measurable, personally relevant targets
3. **Prompts/cues** — Context-aware triggers at the right moment
4. **Self-monitoring** — User-initiated tracking of behavior and outcomes
5. **Reward/incentive** — Immediate positive feedback for completion

## Implications for AI Systems
- AI systems should prioritize these five BCTs in their core loop
- Real-time analytics make feedback and monitoring scalable
- ML prediction enables precisely timed prompts/cues
- Personality data enables personalized goal setting
- Gamification provides reward/incentive infrastructure
