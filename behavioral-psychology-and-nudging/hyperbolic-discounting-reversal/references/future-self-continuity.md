# Future-Self Continuity: Research and Interventions

## Core Definition

Future-self continuity is the degree to which a person feels psychologically connected to their future self. Higher continuity leads to better long-term decision-making. Lower continuity leads to hyperbolic discounting — treating the future self as a stranger whose interests can be discounted.

## Key Research

### Hershfield et al. (2011) — Age-Progressed Avatars
Hershfield, H. E., Goldstein, D. G., Sharpe, W. F., Fox, J., Yeykelis, L., Carstensen, L. L., & Bailenson, J. N. (2011). Increasing saving behavior through age-progressed renderings of the future self. *Journal of Marketing Research*, 48(SPL), S23–S37.

- Participants shown age-progressed avatars of themselves increased retirement savings contributions.
- The intervention worked by making the future self feel real and connected.
- Demonstrated that visualizing the future self reduces temporal discounting.

### Bartels & Urminsky (2011) — Connectedness and Savings
Bartels, D. M., & Urminsky, O. (2011). On intertemporal selfishness: How the perceived instability of identity underlies impatient consumption. *Journal of Consumer Research*, 38(1), 182–198.

- Showed that people who feel their identity is stable over time save more.
- Identity continuity and financial patience are directly linked.

### Hershfield (2011) — Dissociation from Future Self
Hershfield, H. E. (2011). Future self-continuity: How conceptions of the future self transform intertemporal choice. *Topics in Cognitive Science*, 3(2), 341–356.

- People with low future-self continuity show increased delay discounting.
- Interventions that increase continuity reduce impulsive choices.

### Blouin-Hudon & Gagné (2020) — Future Self-Continuity Interventions
Blouin-Hudon, E. M. C., & Gagné, M. (2020). From younger self to older self: A systematic review of the impact of future self-continuity interventions on health and well-being. *Frontiers in Psychology*, 11, 536.

- Review of interventions across domains: finance, health, education, environment.
- Found consistent evidence that increasing future-self continuity improves long-term outcomes.
- Effective techniques include: letter writing, visualization, avatar representation, narrative transportation.

## Techniques for A-Tech

| Technique | Application |
|---|---|
| Future-self letter | Auto-generated letter at checkout showing future benefits of ownership |
| Temporal visualization | Dashboard showing cumulative subscription cost vs. lifetime savings |
| Narrative transportation | "Two futures" chapter in Be Practical: parallel lives of renter vs. owner |
| Identity continuity | "Owner" badge and public pledge reinforcing stable identity |
| Vivid consequences | Making the pain of ongoing subscriptions visceral and personal |

## Key Insight

The most powerful future-self intervention is not a visualization alone — it is a visualization paired with an immediate action. Seeing the future self motivates; acting on that motivation requires a precommitment device. The combination of future-self continuity + precommitment is the complete reversal framework.
