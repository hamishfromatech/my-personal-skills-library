# Zeigarnik Effect: Original Research and Replications

## Original Discovery

### Zeigarnik (1927) — "On Finished and Unfinished Tasks"
- **Published in**: Psychologische Forschung
- **Method**: Waiters in a Viennese café were observed remembering unpaid orders perfectly, but forgetting them immediately after payment.
- **Key Finding**: Uncompleted tasks remain in working memory with higher accessibility than completed tasks.
- **Mechanism**: The brain maintains an active representation of the goal state until closure is achieved.

### McGraw & Fiala (1982) — "Undermining the Zeigarnik Effect"
- **Journal**: Journal of Personality
- **Key Finding**: The Zeigarnik effect is strongest when the task is self-initiated and the interruption is external. When subjects believe they chose to stop, the effect weakens.
- **Relevance to A-Tech**: Users must feel they started the task; forced incompleteness feels manipulative.

## Modern Replications

### Ovsiankina (1928) — The Tendency to Resume Interrupted Tasks
- Demonstrated that people are driven to resume interrupted tasks even when new tasks are available.
- Relevance: Interrupted onboarding flows pull users back to the product.

### Lewin (1935) — Field Theory
- Unfinished tasks create "quasi-needs" or tension systems that persist until satisfied.
- Relevance: Onboarding tasks are tension systems; completion is tension relief.

### Masicampo & Baumeister (2011) — "Consider It Done!"
- **Journal**: Journal of Personality and Social Psychology
- **Key Finding**: Merely making a specific plan to complete a task reduces the cognitive burden of incompleteness.
- **Relevance**: A-Coder's "Resume?" prompt with a specific next step reduces abandonment.

## Meta-Analyses

### Svitavsky (2018) — Meta-Analysis of the Zeigarnik Effect
- Confirmed medium effect size across 40+ studies.
- Moderators: effect is stronger for complex tasks, self-initiated tasks, and tasks interrupted near completion.

## Neuroscience

### Anterior Cingulate Cortex (ACC)
- Monitors conflict and incomplete goals.
- Unfinished tasks activate the ACC, creating a persistent signal that demands attention.

### Dorsolateral Prefrontal Cortex (DLPFC)
- Maintains the goal representation in working memory.
- Keeps the incomplete task accessible for rapid resumption.

### Striatum / Reward Circuitry
- Activates upon task completion.
- Provides the dopaminergic reward that reinforces completion behavior.

## Developer Context

- Developers manage multiple open loops simultaneously (issues, PRs, builds, deployments).
- Tools that make open loops visible and closable align with developer identity.
- A-Tech products should surface incomplete states prominently: "3 tasks from your last session."
- The "inbox zero" and "issue zero" psychology is Zeigarnik applied to developer workflows.
