# Status Quo Bias: Samuelson & Zeckhauser (1988)

## Original Paper

### Samuelson & Zeckhauser — "Status Quo Bias in Decision Making"
- **Published in**: Journal of Risk and Uncertainty, 1988
- **Key Finding**: People disproportionately prefer to maintain their current state, even when alternatives are demonstrably superior.
- **Mechanism**: Three cognitive biases combine to create status quo bias:
  1. **Loss Aversion**: Losses loom larger than gains; staying avoids perceived losses.
  2. **Endowment Effect**: People overvalue what they already have.
  3. **Omission Bias**: Harmful inactions are judged less harshly than harmful actions.

## Relevant Extensions

### Kahneman & Tversky (1979) — Prospect Theory
- **Key Finding**: Value function is asymmetric; losses are weighted ~2x more than equivalent gains.
- **Relevance**: Reversal requires framing the status quo as a loss, not the alternative as a gain.

### Ritov & Baron (1992) — "Status-Quo and Omission Biases"
- Demonstrated omission bias in vaccination and environmental decisions.
- Relevance: Developers who don't switch tools feel they are "not doing anything wrong"—making omission visible is key.

### Burnham, Frels & Mahajan (2003) — "Consumer Switching Costs"
- **Journal**: Journal of the Academy of Marketing Science
- **Key Finding**: Consumers systematically overestimate switching costs, especially for complex products.
- **Relevance**: Reducing *perceived* switching costs (through import tools, config migration) is as important as reducing actual costs.

### Eidelman & Crandall (2012) — "Bias in Favor of the Status Quo"
- **Key Finding**: People prefer the status quo even when it is arbitrarily assigned.
- **Relevance**: Defaults are destiny. Making local-first the default makes it the new status quo.

## Practical Applications for A-Tech

1. **Default Inversion**: A-Coder should default to local mode. Cloud API mode requires active opt-in.
2. **Loss Framing**: Be Practical's calculator should show what developers are *already* losing, not what they would gain.
3. **Switching Cost Reduction**: One-command import tools reduce perceived and actual switching costs simultaneously.
4. **Public Commitment**: Builder's Club challenges make the status quo visible as an unfulfilled promise.
5. **Team Migration**: Provide team-level migration paths, not just individual ones. Team status quo is harder to overcome.

## Ethical Boundaries

- Never invent costs the user is not actually paying.
- Never make the current tool feel dangerous if it is not.
- Always provide a frictionless return path to the old tool.
- The goal is to reveal hidden costs, not manufacture false fears.
