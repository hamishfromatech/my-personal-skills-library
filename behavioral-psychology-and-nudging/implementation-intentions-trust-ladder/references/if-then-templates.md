# If-Then Templates for A-Tech Products

## How to Use These Templates

Each template follows the Gollwitzer format: **If [situation], then [behavior]**.

- The **situation** must be specific, observable, and time-bound.
- The **behavior** must be concrete, actionable, and within the user's control.
- Vague intentions fail. Specific plans succeed.

## The "Scars Not Wounds" Storytelling Format

Use if-then structure to turn past failures into future-ready plans:

**Template:**

> "I used to [vague, failed intention]. The result was [specific wound]. The scar I carry is [specific lesson]. Now, my if-then plan is: If [situation], then [behavior]."

**Example:**

> "I used to say 'I'll learn local AI someday.' The result was six months of paying for cloud tools that trained on my code. The scar I carry is that 'someday' is a trap. Now, my if-then plan is: If I sit down at my desk on Monday morning, then I'll spend the first 20 minutes with the A-Coder tutorial before opening any other tool."
