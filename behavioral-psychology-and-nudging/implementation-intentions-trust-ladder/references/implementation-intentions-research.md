# Implementation Intentions: Research Foundations

## Core Research

### Gollwitzer & Brandstätter (1997)
**Implementation Intentions and Effective Goal Pursuit**
- Original framework: "If [situation], then I will [behavior]"
- Implementation intentions help close the gap between goal setting and goal achievement
- Effective because they delegate control to situational cues, reducing demands on conscious attention

### Gollwitzer & Sheeran (2006)
**Meta-Analysis of Implementation Intentions**
- Analyzed 94 independent studies (N > 8,000)
- Implementation intentions produced medium-to-large effect sizes (d = 0.65) on goal achievement
- Effects strongest when:
  - The goal is challenging (not trivial)
  - The person has strong goal commitment
  - The if-then plan is specific about situation AND behavior

### Gollwitzer, Bieleke, & Sheeran (2016)
**Enhancing Consumer Behaviour with Implementation Intentions**
- Applied to marketing and consumer behavior contexts
- If-then plans increase follow-through on purchase intentions, health behaviors, and financial decisions
- Key insight: Implementation intentions don't just increase behavior—they protect goal pursuit from distractions and competing temptations

## Key Mechanisms

1. **Cue Detection Enhancement**
   - The brain becomes hypersensitive to the specified situation
   - Automatic scanning for the "if" trigger

2. **Response Readiness**
   - The "then" behavior is pre-decided
   - No deliberation needed in the moment
   - Bypasses ego depletion and decision fatigue

3. **Goal Shielding**
   - Implementation intentions protect the goal from competing responses
   - "If I feel tempted to use the cloud IDE, then I'll remember my proprietary code risk."

4. **Automatic Initiation**
   - The behavior becomes linked to the situation at an automatic level
   - Reduces reliance on willpower and motivation

## For Product/Marketing Teams

### Writing Effective If-Then Plans

| Weak | Strong |
|------|--------|
| "If I have time, I'll try the tool." | "If I finish lunch at my desk tomorrow, I'll install A-Coder and run the demo." |
| "If I need help, I'll ask the community." | "If I'm stuck for more than 15 minutes, I'll post my question in #troubleshooting with a screenshot." |
| "If I like it, I'll buy the playbook." | "If I save 2+ hours this week using the IDE, I'll purchase the Playbook Edition before Friday." |

### Critical Components
1. **Specific situation** — When EXACTLY? (time, place, preceding event)
2. **Specific behavior** — What EXACTLY? (observable action, not vague intention)
3. **Strong goal commitment** — The user must actually want the outcome
