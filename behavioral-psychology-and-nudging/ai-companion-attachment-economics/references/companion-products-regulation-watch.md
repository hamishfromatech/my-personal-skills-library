## 2. Regulatory Landscape (watch list)

- **US federal:** ongoing congressional attention to companion-bot safety for minors; industry norms forming around "no romantic companions for minors" as the de-facto standard.
- **US state-level:** 2026 state bills patterned on minors-protection and deception-prevention frames (e.g., "no anthropomorphic deception" language is migrating from neurorights bills into companion-bot bills).
- **EU:** AI Act transparency obligations (Article 50 synthetic content labeling; emotion-recognition restrictions) apply to companion products; the "emotionally manipulative AI" framing is live. Track Digital Services Act (DSA) engagement-design exposure analog — K.G.M. v. Meta is the US analog; DSA addictive-design enforcement (TikTok preliminary finding, Feb 2026) is the EU track.
- **Australia:** under-16 social media ban (from 2024 law) — companion products face analogous access restrictions under minors-protection framings.

**Design implication:** consent-first, minors-safe, no-anthropomorphic-deception defaults make the product *regulation-proof*. The cost of designing for the worst-case regulator is low; the exposure at scale is catastrophic.

## 3. Three Monetization Postures

| Posture | Mechanism | Risk profile | A-Tech fit |
|---|---|---| All three postures monetize, but postures 2-3 are the only defensible long-term positions for A-Tech. Posture 1 inherits the social-media regulatory risk transfer. |
| **1. Engagement-farm (dark)** | Ad-funded attention; engagement-optimized persona | Litigation + regulatory exposure (see K.G.M. line); trust collapse | ✗ |
| **2. Consent-based monetization** | Paid tools, paid no-ads, paid privacy, paid memory | Lower revenue ceiling, durable trust asset | ✓ (primary recommendation) |
| **3. Capability/premium tools** | Deeper memory, richer context, integrations, faster tiers | Modest revenue ceiling; sustainable | ✓ (complement to posture 2) |

## 4. Product Design Checklist (four gates)

1. **Business-model gate:** revenue from tools/subscription/consent tiers; no ad-funded attention
2. **Memory-continuity gate:** persistent memory as moat, but exportable, user-owned, deletable
3. **Safety-instrumentation gate:** attach the attachment-footprint signals (SKILL.md Section 2); quarterly aggregate trust-report
4. **Openness gate:** open-source the persona spec, keep hosted inference as the monetized layer
