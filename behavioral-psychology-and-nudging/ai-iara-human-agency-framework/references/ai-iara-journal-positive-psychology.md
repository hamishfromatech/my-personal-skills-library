# The AI-IARA Framework: How to Cultivate Human Agency Before Artificial Conditions Erode It

**Source:** Journal of Positive Psychology, 2026  
**Authors:** Llewellyn E. Van Zyl et al.  
**URL:** https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/17439760.2026.2632939

## Framework Summary
The AI-IARA framework identifies six interconnected human capacities essential for wellbeing under algorithmic conditions:
1. **Intentionality** — Acting with purpose rather than algorithmic nudging
2. **Awareness** — Metacognitive monitoring of one's own thinking with AI
3. **Resilience** — Recovery from AI errors and cognitive overload
4. **Authenticity** — Maintaining genuine self-expression against homogenization
5. **Relatedness** — Genuine human connection alongside AI interaction
6. **Adaptability** — Learning and evolving as AI capabilities change

## Key Insight
The paper argues that AI systems are not neutral tools—they actively shape human psychological states. Current design practices unintentionally erode these capacities. The framework provides "a translation of psychological science into engineering specifications."
