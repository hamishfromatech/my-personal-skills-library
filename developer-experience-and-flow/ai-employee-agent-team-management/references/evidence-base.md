## The Tool-to-Teammate Shift

### Agentic AI Definition (2026 Consensus)
- **Google Cloud:** "advanced form of artificial intelligence focused on autonomous decision-making and action. Unlike traditional AI, which primarily responds to commands or analyzes data, agentic AI..."
- **IBM:** "artificial intelligence system that can accomplish a specific goal with limited supervision. It consists of ai agents—machine learning models that mimic human decision-making"
- **MIT Sloan (Feb 2026):** "attention has shifted to the next evolution of generative AI: AI agents or agentic AI, a new breed of AI systems that are semi- or fully autonomous and thus able to perceive, reason, and act"
- **Wikipedia:** "AI agents (also referred to as compound AI systems or agentic AI) are a class of intelligent agents that can pursue goals, use tools, and take actions with limited supervision"
- **GeeksforGeeks (May 2026):** "AI system that can autonomously make decisions, plan actions and execute tasks to achieve specific goals with minimal human intervention"

### Enterprise Adoption Evidence
- **AP News (Nov 2025):** "AI agents, by contrast, are supposed to be able to take actions autonomously on a person's behalf"
- **Cyber.gov.au (May 2026):** "Agentic AI systems are composed of one or more agents that fundamentally rely on an AI model, such as an LLM, to interpret and reason about the state of the world, make decisions and take actions"
- **iTMunch (2026):** "The Rise of the 'AI Employee': Why Enterprises Are Starting to Manage Agents Like Teams"
- **Agentic.ai (2026):** Six levels of agentic AI autonomy defined

### Why the Shift Is Happening
1. **Autonomy increase** — agents pursue goals, use tools, take multi-step actions
2. **Persistent identity** — agents maintain context across sessions
3. **Specialization** — role-specific agents (code review, deployment, security)
4. **Collaboration patterns** — agents work alongside humans with defined handoffs
5. **Accountability requirements** — organizations need management structures for agent actions
