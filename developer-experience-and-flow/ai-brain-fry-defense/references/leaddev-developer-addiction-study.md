# LeadDev Developer Addiction Study — Extraction

## Source
LeadDev — "'Addictive' agentic coding has developers losing sleep" (Sage Lazzaro, March 30, 2026)

## Key Data Points
- Multitudes study (500+ developers): 19.6% rise in out-of-hours commits among AI tool users
- ActivTrak Productivity Lab (163,638 employees, Jan 2023–Dec 2025): AI drove 46% increase in Saturday productive hours and 58% increase in Sunday productive hours
- High performers adopted AI and did more — not the same amount more efficiently
- Sharp increase in messaging (+145%), collaboration (+5%), and business management tool time (+94%)

## Developer Perspectives

### Eren Celebi (WPP)
- "I'm coding into later hours of the day not because I'm told to do so, but because I can't get myself to get up from the computer."
- At worst, AI coding makes it hard to understand his own work
- Chases AI for answers "like a junior engineer would send Slack messages to the senior"

### Doug Sims (Paramount)
- More productive than ever, but also far busier
- "I feel compelled to be doing side projects and learning constantly. The weekends just disappear."
- Positive reinforcement drives taking on more work through nights and weekends
- "It feels like I'm onto something new every day."

### Rajesh Padmakumaran (Genpact)
- Rapid scaffolding creation creates powerful sense of momentum
- Developers get into flow state faster but with added cognitive overhead
- Those who enjoy AI coding the most feel the most cognitive load

### Alon Sabi (SREDify)
- Agentic coding freed him to step away from screen because he can think architecturally while walking
- Individual contributors face a significant shift: managing agents is new skill
- "We need to understand there's a limit to how much we can grasp as human beings."

## A-Tech Implications
- The weekend work increase (+46% Saturday, +58% Sunday) is a health crisis, not a productivity win
- Tools must actively enforce rest boundaries, not just enable more output
- The "flow state" described is often toxic flow — momentum without comprehension
- A-Coder's Flow Guardian should distinguish between healthy flow and addictive overdrive
