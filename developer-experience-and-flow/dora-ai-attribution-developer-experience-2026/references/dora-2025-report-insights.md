# DORA 2025 State of AI-Assisted Software Development: Detailed Insights

## Source
DORA / Google Cloud — "Balancing AI Tensions: Moving from AI adoption to effective SDLC use"
Jessica Baolin & Nathen Harvey, March 10, 2026

## Research Methodology
Thematic deep dive into 1,110 open-ended survey responses from Google software engineers in Q3 2025. Engineers shared thoughts on how AI-powered tools impacted development workflows over the previous three months.

## The Seven Team Archetypes (2025)
Instead of simple performance tiers, the report introduced seven team archetypes combining delivery, stability, and well-being patterns:
1. **Elite Performers:** High delivery, high stability, sustainable well-being
2. **High Performers:** Good delivery, good stability, manageable pressure
3. **Overachievers:** High delivery, high stability, unsustainable pressure
4. **Steady Performers:** Moderate delivery, good stability, comfortable pace
5. **At-Risk Performers:** Moderate delivery, poor stability, rising burnout
6. **Challenged Teams:** Poor delivery, poor stability, systemic dysfunction
7. **Emerging AI Adopters:** Inconsistent delivery, experimenting with AI, unclear outcomes

## The DORA AI Capabilities Model
Seven capabilities that predict whether AI stabilizes or destabilizes delivery:
1. Clear and communicated AI stance
2. Healthy data ecosystems
3. AI-accessible internal data
4. Strong version control practices
5. User-centric focus
6. Robust feedback loops
7. AI governance

## Key Quotes from Engineers
- "I feel somewhat more productive, but it's at a cost. While I end up spending less time writing code, I spend more time babysitting the AI and reviewing what it is trying to do."
- "Reviewing [another's] code is so much harder than writing it. AI tools are increasing the rate at which people can churn out code that needs to be reviewed..."
- "AI powered development has allowed me to develop more quickly in areas I don't have experience in, but that means that I occasionally get a sense of 'the blind leading the blind' where I don't have enough context to ensure that the AI is correct."
- "I get frozen in front of a blank page. Giving me a place to dump ideas and refine them has made [AI tools] key parts of my workflow."

## Critical Statistic
30% of developers currently report little to no trust in the code generated by AI.
