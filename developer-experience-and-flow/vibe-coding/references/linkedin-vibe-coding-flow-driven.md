# Reference: Vibe Coding Explained — LinkedIn
**Source:** https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/vibe-coding-explained-new-era-flow-driven-development-rajendren-camnc
**Date:** May 2025
**Key Points:**

- Flow-to-Feature Framework (F2F): Tune In → Ride the Flow → Review with Reality → Log & Learn
- Tools: Raycast, Zed, ChatGPT as co-creators
- Ambient setups that optimize dopamine-driven productivity
- Inspired by "Flow" by Mihaly Csikszentmihalyi and "The Creative Act" by Rick Rubin
- Pros: boosts creativity, faster prototyping, AI-assisted clarity
- Cons: lack of structure, not ideal for scaling without conventions, hard to measure by traditional metrics
- Best for: startup founders, indie developers, creative coders, AI-native developers
- Not ideal for: highly regulated, enterprise, or critical systems (yet)
- Vibe coding isn't anti-discipline; it's pro-flow
