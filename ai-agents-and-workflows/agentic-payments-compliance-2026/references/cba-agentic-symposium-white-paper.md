# CBA Agentic Symposium White Paper Summary

## Source
- **Title:** Symposium on Agentic AI in Payments
- **Publisher:** Consumer Bankers Association (CBA)
- **Date:** January 2026
- **URL:** https://consumerbankers.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/CBA-Agentic-Symposium-White-Paper-2026-01v2.pdf

## Key Findings

### 1. Agentic Commerce Catalyst
Agentic commerce could be the catalyst A2A (account-to-account) payments have been waiting for. AI agents are natural consumers of real-time payment infrastructure because they:
- Require machine-readable payment instructions
- Benefit from instant settlement
- Operate programmatically without human-per-transaction friction

### 2. Liability Allocation Questions
The white paper identifies unresolved questions in agentic payment liability:
- If agentic payments generate disputes, merchants could face liability for fraud
- The line between agent error and merchant failure is unclear
- "Who owns the mistake?" — user, agent developer, merchant, or platform?

### 3. Regulatory Sandbox Opportunity
CBA recommends coordinated industry-regulator sandbox experiments to:
- Test mandate-based authorization models at scale
- Develop standardized dispute resolution protocols
- Establish agent identity verification benchmarks
- Measure consumer protection outcomes vs. traditional payments

### 4. US Regulatory Guidance Gap
US regulatory guidance on agent liability for payment remains fragmented:
- No federal banking regulator has issued specific guidance
- State-level money transmission laws may apply unevenly
- UDAAP (Unfair, Deceptive, or Abusive Acts or Practices) remains a catch-all risk
