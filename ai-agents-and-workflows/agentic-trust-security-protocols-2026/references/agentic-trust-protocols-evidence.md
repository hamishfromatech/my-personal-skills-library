# Agentic Trust & Security Protocols 2026 — Evidence Base

## Primary Sources

### Visa Trusted Agent Protocol (TAP)
- **Visa Inc. (October 14, 2025)** — "Visa Introduces Trusted Agent Protocol: An Ecosystem-Led Framework for AI Commerce." Press release. San Francisco. Available at investor.visa.com and GitHub (visa/trusted-agent-protocol).
- **Key data:** 4,700% surge in AI-driven traffic to US retail sites (Adobe Data Insights, Aug 2025). 85% of AI shoppers say it improved their experience.
- **Partners at launch:** Cloudflare, Adyen, Ant International, Checkout.com, Coinbase, CyberSource, Elavon, Fiserv, Microsoft, Nuvei, Shopify, Stripe, Worldpay.
- **Technical foundation:** HTTP Message Signatures standard, aligned with WebAuthn. No-code functionality for merchants. Extensible to non-web protocols.
- **Interoperability commitments:** IETF, OpenID Foundation, EMVCo. Aligning with Agentic Commerce Protocol (ACP) and Coinbase/x402.
- **Quote:** Jack Forestell (Chief Product & Strategy Officer, Visa): "We believe the entire payments ecosystem has a responsibility to ensure sellers can trust AI agents as much as they trust their best customers and networks."

### FIDO Alliance Agentic Authentication Standards
- **FIDO Alliance (April 28, 2026)** — "FIDO Alliance to Develop Standards for Trusted AI Agent Interactions." Press release.
- **Working Groups:**
  - Agentic Authentication Technical WG: Chaired by CVS Health, Google, OpenAI. Vice-chaired by Amazon, Google, Okta.
  - Payments Technical WG: Chaired by Mastercard, Visa.
- **Founding contributions:**
  - Google AP2 (Agent Payments Protocol): Model for secure delegation, verifiable authorization, trusted transaction execution. Quote from Stavan Parikh (VP/GM Payments, Google): "Contributing AP2 to a trusted industry association like the FIDO Alliance ensures it stays open, platform-agnostic, and community-led."
  - Mastercard Verifiable Intent: Co-developed with Google, works with AP2. Quote from Pablo Fourez (Chief Digital Officer, Mastercard): "By contributing Verifiable Intent to the FIDO Alliance's standards work... we're supporting an approach that creates a shared record of user intent that the entire payments ecosystem can rely on."
- **Three focus areas:** Verifiable User Instructions, Agent Authentication, Trusted Delegation for Commerce.
- **Board member support statements:** 1Password, American Express (ACE Developer Kit), Dashlane, Google, LastPass, Mastercard, OneSpan, PayPal, Prove Identity, Thales, Visa.
- **Market estimate:** Agentic commerce could reach $5 trillion globally by 2030.

### Experian Agent Trust
- **Experian (April 30, 2026)** — "Experian Announces Agent Trust to Power Trusted AI Driven Commerce." Press release.
- **Partners:** Visa, Cloudflare, Skyfire (open KYA protocol), Akamai (added June 2026).
- **Token validates:** Identity, consent, delegated authority, transaction risk — in real time.
- **Positioning:** "Know Your Agent" (KYA) framework. Human-to-agent binding service.
- **Quote:** "Agentic commerce will not scale without trust."
- **Forbes coverage (Tim Keary, May 1, 2026):** "Experian announced the launch of Agent Trust, a human-to-agent binding service that binds verified human identities with the AI agents acting on their behalf."

### Akamai Agentic Security Framework
- **Akamai (June 15, 2026)** — "Akamai Unveils Agentic Security Framework to Power Trusted AI-Driven Interactions and Commerce." Press release.
- **Features:** Know Your Agent (KYA) protocol, agent identity verification, human delegation verification.
- **Integration:** With Visa's Trusted Agent Protocol. Defines how agents operate in payment environments.

### Mastercard Agent Pay + Verifiable Intent
- **Mastercard (June 10, 2026)** — "Mastercard launches Agent Pay for Machines."
- **Features:** Card-network tokenization. Scoped spending tokens with spending limits, merchant whitelists, time windows, human revocation.
- **Partners:** Microsoft, Block, IBM, Adyen.
- **Verifiable Intent:** Co-developed with Google. Contributed to FIDO Alliance (April 2026). Creates shared record of user intent.

- A2A (Agent-to-Agent) → 150+ organizations adopted by April 2026

### The KYA Pattern
The Know Your Agent framework appears across multiple protocols with consistent four pillars:
1. **Provenance** — who created the agent, is it trusted
2. **Authorization** — has the human authorized it, what are the boundaries
3. **Accountability** — can actions be traced to a responsible party
4. **Traceability** — is there a verifiable record of what was done and why
