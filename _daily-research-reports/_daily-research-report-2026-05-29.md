## Strategic Implications for A-Tech

1. **Context-maxxing is the competitive moat.** The Brookings paper validates A-Tech's entire thesis: user-controlled, open-source AI deployments expand cognitive agency while vendor-controlled interfaces erode it. A-Coder should explicitly position as a "context-maxxing IDE."

2. **Proof-first design differentiates against black-box competitors.** While Copilot and Cursor hide their reasoning, A-Coder can make every agent action inspectable, accountable, and correctable.

3. **AI-IARA provides a compliance-ready design philosophy.** As EU AI Act and global AI regulations expand, having a published psychological-safety framework embedded in product design reduces regulatory risk.

4. **Consolidation is the DevEx battleground.** The market is consolidating from 5-10 AI tools per developer to 1-2 integrated environments. A-Coder must win by being the single tool that replaces the stack.

5. **Privacy-first personalization is table stakes by 2026.** Zero-party data, synthetic data, federated learning, and differential privacy should be standard infrastructure, not premium features.
