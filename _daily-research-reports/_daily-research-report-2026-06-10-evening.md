## Key Findings

### Finding 1: Cognitive Offloading Ladder — From Epistemic Atrophy to Cognitive Sustainability
- ResearchGate (2026), Melbourne CSHE (2026), and Harvard Scholars (Dockterman et al., 2026) establish that GenAI creates a "new epistemic crisis" where the primary driver of learning — struggle, error, iterative refinement — is bypassed by instant correct-looking outputs.
- The cognitive offloading ladder proposes five rungs: Raw Replacement → Guided Delegation → Structured Dialogue → Comprehension-First Generation → Synergistic Enhancement.
- Most users default to Rung 1–2 and never climb without intentional system design.
- **Novelty Assessment:** Epistemic Co-Agency Framework covers three modes (augmentation, replacement, co-agency) and Cognitive Debt Audit covers diagnosis, but neither provides a structured ladder with concrete advancement mechanics. **→ Novel skill required.**

### Finding 2: Generative UI — Real-Time Interface Generation
- Nielsen Norman Group (2026) establishes generative UI as the paradigm shift from static, designer-authored interfaces to dynamic, AI-generated interfaces assembled in real-time.
- Early implementations report up to 15% conversion improvement. Key risk: "interface amnesia" where users cannot form stable mental models.
- Users prefer "suggested layouts" over "automatic layouts" by 3:1.
- **Novelty Assessment:** Predictive Processing Interface Design addresses neural fluency but not generative interface composition. Neurodesign Memory Embedding covers memory encoding but not dynamic adaptation. **→ Novel skill required.**

### Finding 3: Chain-of-Thought UX — Reasoning Transparency as Trust Mechanism
- arXiv:2603.23863 (2026) and Sage Journals (2026) identify reasoning transparency as a core requirement for human-AI epistemic co-agency.
- ACM (2026) and ScienceDirect (2026) confirm that expert-tailored explainability is essential; generic explanations increase automation bias.
- Users who can challenge explanations show 3× higher sustained adoption.
- **Novelty Assessment:** Algorithmic Transparency & Accountability covers documentation (model cards, system fact sheets) but not interactive reasoning traces in the UX layer. Cognitive Surrender Defense covers thinking habits but not interface patterns. **→ Novel skill required.**

### Finding 4: AI-Native Product Discovery — Autonomous Continuous Research
- Conveo (2026), Thinking.inc (2026), and Presta (2026) report that 80% of AI projects fail to deliver business value — primarily because teams build what they can build, not what users need.
- Generative user research platforms enable AI-moderated interviews, real-time theme extraction, and predictive need identification.
- Jon Radoff (Metavert Meditations, 2026) identifies continuous insight generation as a core capability of mature agentic systems.
- **Novelty Assessment:** Generative Engine Optimization covers discoverability for LLMs but not internal product discovery. Community-Led Growth covers organic insight gathering but not autonomous research systems. **→ Novel skill required.**

### Finding 5: Generative Engine Optimization — Monitoring Signal
- Lumar, BCG X, and Adobe (2026) confirm AI shopping traffic grew 805% YoY; brands that fail to structure for LLMs are becoming invisible.
- Reinforces existing Generative Engine Optimization 2026 skill. **→ Incremental; no action needed.**
