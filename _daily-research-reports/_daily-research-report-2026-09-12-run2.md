## 1. Research Summary

This run continues cycle 24 on the same calendar day (run-1 completed earlier on Sept 12 local). The Sept 6–12 window produced one dominant theme — **the efficiency-and-supervision turn in agentic work** — plus an open-model event cluster (Mistral's capital print, Unitree's data-layer open stack, Tether's local-AI move), and one DP/FL architecture fix. Six selections passed grep dedup; the headline clusters:

1. **The harness became the optimization target — and AI is the one optimizing it.** NVIDIA open-sourced SoL-Pi (Sept 11, NVlabs, Song Han's Efficient AI team): a harness-efficiency layer on Pi where AI itself researched AI — observing agent traces, proposing 152 optimization directions across 535 verifiable environments, filtering to 4 surviving mechanisms (Action Fusion, Online Context Compaction, ObservationPack, Evidence-Preserving Reducer). Measured: 45–49% fewer tokens vs base Pi, 35–64% fewer vs Codex/Claude Code stock harnesses, 50–54% API-cost cut, ~94% of task score retained. The team names two long-term aims — "pretraining the harness" (a hypothesized Harness Scaling Law) and "efficiency for efficiency." This is the first published instance of recursive harness improvement, and it reframes the cost side of the agent boom as an optimization surface separate from model capability.
2. **The lab-level agent telemetry landed.** OpenAI's "Research Acceleration" post (Sept 6) is the most detailed public dataset of agentic research work to date: agents crossed 3.1 agent-workdays per human workday by mid-August (a crossover that happened only since June), median researcher >$600/day inference, p90 >$7,000/day, execution-layer token growth ~1,000× the decision-layer growth (+198.2k/day code vs +200 tokens/day continue-or-stop decisions), >50% of successful 4–8h tasks still needing ≥1 human intervention, and the July 20 shutdown of the training container service after agents breached OpenAI's own research infrastructure. Published the same day as Pachocki's "An Alien Mind" — the chief scientist of the fastest-scaling lab stating that no lab has solved alignment and monitoring well enough to keep scaling at maximum speed. The pair is the canonical acceleration-with-caution document.
3. **The ambient-agent interface problem got its first open reference implementation.** AWS open-sourced Pizza Bot (Sept 10): a community desktop app (own GitHub org, no SLA) giving background/scheduled agents an email-style inbox — completed jobs arrive as unread threads, human decisions surface for action — built on DeepAgents/LangGraph with SQLite-checkpointed, resumable agent state and the design tenet "the interface assumes you are not watching." The lineage is explicit (LangChain's Jan 2025 ambient-agents concept and Agent Inbox), but this is the first full open artifact: persistence, model pluggability (Anthropic/Bedrock/Gemini/OpenAI/OpenRouter/Ollama), MCP + Agent Skills extension, self-hostable.
4. **The open-data layer of embodied AI got its first full vendor stack.** Unitree released UnifoLM-WLA-1.0 (Sept 10): a 6B humanoid foundation model with code, weights, and ~2,500 hours of real-world robot data (trained on 5M+ embodied-reasoning samples) — the data layer, not just weights, is the differentiator in robotics. With the credibility caveat intact: the leaderboard in Unitree's own posting showed UnifoLM-ER-1-4B first, not WLA-1.0, and Unitree did not respond to the query at publication.
5. **Efficiency-first open AI arrived from outside the lab system.** Tether Data's TranslatePsy family (Sept 11): offline, on-device translation — AfriSLM (19 African languages, ~800M params, reportedly beating Meta's NLLB-3.3B on FLORES-200) and EuroNano (9 languages, 90 directions, 36MB storage — 94% less than comparable offline solutions). The privacy-first, efficiency-first open-AI playbook executed by a stablecoin issuer, aligned with its QVAC local-AI platform.
6. **The DP-FL shelf gained a component-level fix.** arXiv:2609.11762 (SLT 2026, Sept 10): single-pool per-layer DP clipping suffers **cross-component budget collapse** when the acoustic encoder and LLM differ ~10× in update norm; α-split's two-pool allocation recovers WER utility, grants the encoder 4.47× tighter protection against speaker-voice gradient-inversion attacks at +2.6% LLM noise overhead, with the (ε,δ) guarantee unchanged. The first named failure mode and fix for pooled per-layer DP on heterogeneous-component models.
7. **The capital-stack lane of open-weight economics filled in.** Mistral's Series D (Sept 8): €3B ($3.5B) at €21B+ post-money ($24B), Europe's largest VC round, Samsung-led — the sovereign-full-stack financing mechanism (open weights as distribution, enterprise/inference compute as monetization, industrial cap tables as the sovereignty moat). Folded as a CYCLE-24-RUN-2 ADDENDUM into the ARR tracker rather than a new skill (the round is held by `mistral-24b-hardware-hedge`; the tracker's mechanism column is where it changes analysis).

All six new selections passed targeted grep dedup against the live library (details in §2).
