## Cross-Domain Observations

### The "Library Maturity" Signal
This cycle's research confirms that the A-Tech skill library has reached a high degree of coverage maturity. Of approximately 25 distinct search results across six domains, only 2 (8%) were genuinely novel. The remaining 92% were either already captured or incremental confirmations. This suggests:
1. The daily research process is effectively capturing the research frontier.
2. Future cycles should consider deeper retrieval (fetching full papers rather than relying on search snippets) to find novel findings that may not appear in search result summaries.
3. The library may benefit from periodic "gap analysis" cycles that systematically identify what's NOT yet covered rather than what's new.

### The "Co-Design" Theme
Both novel skills this cycle share a structural insight: the most impactful 2026 research is moving from isolated mechanism design to **joint co-design**:
- DAG-AF2L: co-designs Byzantine filtering + DP noise + async staleness (vs. addressing each in isolation)
- DevEx Metrics Compass: co-designs metric selection + context + maturity (vs. imposing a single framework)

This echoes a pattern visible across the library: the field is maturing from "does this mechanism work?" to "how do these mechanisms work together?"
