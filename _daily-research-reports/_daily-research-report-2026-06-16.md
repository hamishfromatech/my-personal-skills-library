## Key Findings

### Finding 1: AI Brain Fry — BCG Confirms Multi-Agent Cognitive Collapse (HIGH PRIORITY)

**Source:** Harvard Business Review — "When Using AI Leads to 'Brain Fry'" (March 5, 2026), co-authored by BCG Managing Directors Julie Bedard and Matthew Kropp with UC Riverside researchers.

**Key data:**
- Productivity peaks at 3 concurrent agents, declines at 4
- 33% more decision fatigue, 39% more major errors, 34% intent to quit at 4+ agents
- Workers using AI during lunch/commutes/breaks accumulate "continuous involvement"
- Managers answering AI questions reduce team mental fatigue by 15%

**Synthesis:** This is an incremental update to `ai-brain-fry-defense` and `agentic-coding-addiction-defense`, but with a new angle: **manager-mediated intervention** reduces fatigue measurably. The BCG data also validates the three-agent ceiling as a hard limit, not a soft recommendation.

**Novelty:** Partially novel — the core finding (3-agent limit) is already captured, but the manager-intervention effect and the Gas Town / Steve Yegge orchestration anecdote are new texture.

### Finding 3: Open Source Monetization Reality — Ground-Truth Revenue Benchmarks

**Source:** DEV Community — ZNY, "Open Source Software Monetization: How Developers Are Actually Making Money in 2026" (May 2026)

**Key data:**
- Median open-source project generates no revenue
- Top 5% generate the majority of revenue
- Donation-based ceiling for popular projects: $1K–$10K/mo
- Open core ceiling: $10K–$100K/mo
- SaaS wrapper ceiling: $50K–$500K/mo
- Solo consultant rate: $100–$500/hour; at 20 hrs/week = $8K–$40K/mo
- The 1% Rule: ~1% of unique downloads convert to paying customers

**Synthesis:** The existing `sustainable-open-source-business-model` skill covers the VictoriaMetrics model and the 1% Rule abstractly, but lacks honest revenue range data by project scale. The DEV Community article provides the missing ground-truth benchmarks that maintainers actually need.

**Novelty:** HIGH — this is a new skill creation: `open-source-monetization-reality-2026`. The combination of revenue benchmarks by scale + model combination strategy + license-as-strategy framework is not currently represented in the skill library.
