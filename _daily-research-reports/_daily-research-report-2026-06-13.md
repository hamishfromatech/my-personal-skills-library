## 1. Research Scan Summary

### Domain A: Augmented Coding Reality Protocol — Evidence Against Vibe Coding

Today's research synthesized the SoftwareSeni comprehensive analysis of independent 2026 research on AI coding tool productivity claims versus measured reality. Key findings:

- **The Perception-Reality Gap:** METR's randomized controlled trial found experienced developers were 19% slower with AI tools despite feeling 20% faster — a 39-percentage-point gap, the largest METR has documented in productivity research.
- **Quality Degradation at Scale:** GitClear's longitudinal analysis of 211 million lines found refactoring collapsed from 25% to under 10% of changed lines, duplication increased 4×, and copy-paste code exceeded moved code for the first time in history.
- **Security Crisis:** CodeRabbit found AI-generated PRs had 2.74× more security vulnerabilities; Tenzai found 100% of 15 vibe-coded production apps lacked CSRF protection and security headers; Veracode found 45% of AI-generated code contains vulnerabilities.
- **The Augmented Coding Alternative:** Kent Beck's disciplined framework (TDD, mandatory review, architectural oversight) and Simon Willison's "vibe engineering" (11 practices for accountable LLM collaboration) provide sustainable pathways.

Novel gap requiring a unified evidence-based decision protocol.

### Domain B: Open Source Funding Crisis Defense — From Charity to Risk Management

Research into DevX and LinuxInsider coverage revealed the 2026 funding emergency has escalated beyond awareness into actionable enterprise risk:

- **Less than 10%** of widely used open-source projects have any dedicated funding model (Linux Foundation 2026)
- **60%** of maintainers receive no payment; **44%** report burnout (Tidelift/Byteiota)
- **Express.js** powers 17 million weekly downloads with approximately one primary maintainer
- **Regulatory shift:** EU Cyber Resilience Act, US Executive Orders, and insurance underwriting now mandate open-source accountability
- **AI noise compounds crisis:** Maintainer burden increased 40–80% due to AI-generated bug reports, PRs, and documentation churn

**Cross-reference:** `monetization-and-revenue/open-source-pledge-sustainability` covers the pledge mechanism but not the emergency triage response. `monetization-and-revenue/open-source-sustainability-infrastructure` covers governance but not the crisis-defense framework. Novel gap requiring immediate-action protocols.

### Domain C: Agentic Payments Ecosystem — Protocol Maturation

Research confirmed AP2, x402, ACP, and MPP are all production-ready with growing adoption:

- **Google AP2:** 60+ partners including Stripe, Visa, Mastercard, PayPal, Coinbase; mandate architecture for human-in-the-loop authorization
- **Coinbase x402:** Zero protocol fees; machine-to-machine native; ideal for API microtransactions
- **Stripe/Tempo MPP:** Session-based micropayment streaming; bridges crypto and fiat
- **Mastercard Agent Pay:** Tokenization infrastructure with Microsoft, Block, IBM, Adyen

**Cross-reference:** `ai-agents-and-workflows/agentic-payments-protocol-ap2` covers the protocol layer. `ai-agents-and-workflows/agentic-commerce-trust-design` covers trust architecture. `monetization-and-revenue/ai-agent-monetization-2026` covers monetization models.
