## 1. Research Summary

Run 2 operates on the same calendar day as run 1 (run-1 completed ~09:20 NZT; this run ~09:45 NZT), so the research window narrows to the last 24–48 hours of the standing domains. The window produced three clusters: (a) a **nudge-persistence doublet** — the ReStud formalization of the 38-experiment persistence finding plus the attention-stock scheduling paper that completes the during-treatment/post-treatment pair; (b) a **creator-capital markets** print that turns creator platform revenue into an underwritable asset class; and (c) a **protocol-native funding + agent-identity** wave (OpenSats + Buzz on Nostr) that extends the ARR tracker's funding-source and identity columns. Two sweep-1 model candidates (Nex-N2.5-Max, Cohere North Small Translate) were eliminated on duplicate-grep. The clusters:

1. **Nudge persistence got its mechanism decomposition.** Brandon, Ferraro, List, Metcalfe, Price & Rundhammer (Review of Economic Studies, Jan 31, 2026, rdag012) formalize the research design for uncovering *why* nudges persist — habit formation vs technology adoption — using resident move-out as a natural discontinuation instrument across **38 natural field experiments**. Headline: fully **half of energy-consumption reductions persist in the home after treatment ends**, consonant with the technology-adoption channel. The library held the cycle-11 working-paper version of this finding (`nudge-persistence-technology-adoption`); the ReStud print is the peer-reviewed formalization with the two-channel decomposition + move-out test as the discriminator. The design upgrade: persistence is a property of **artifacts, not willpower** — budget interventions as artifact cost + nudge cost, not nudge cost alone.
2. **Attention stocks complete the persistence stack.** Byrne, Goette, Martin, Miles, Jones, Schob, Staake & Tiefenbeck (SSRN 3974371, rev. Nov 2024; 700-household shower field experiment, 7 cycling conditions, 86,376 household-shower observations) show feedback effects are **asymmetric** — immediate onset, stable while on, gradual decay after off (+80 ml/shower), and the attention stock builds fast (αON≈0.081, half-life 9 showers) but decays slowly (αOFF≈0.021, half-life 33 showers; effects persist ~59 showers ≈ 2 months). The (I,S,s) counterfactual rule (initial ~17-shower build-up, then cycling to hold attention in [0.700–0.719]) beats the naive exhaust-budget rule by **~15%** under the same budget. Intermittent cycling schedules (T4/T5: 24/48, 12/24) beat comparable-constant schedules on cumulative savings despite equal on-time. Pairing this with Brandon et al. closes the loop: attention stocks decay after treatment (this skill), artifacts persist after it (`nudge-persistence-meta-analysis-38-experiments`) — the full design is cycle-to-maintain + artifact-to-keep.
3. **Creator income became financeable.** CreatorFi raised **$45M (Sept 2, 2026, BI exclusive + coingape press release)** in combined debt+equity (EV3-led equity; prior $12M credit facility 2025) with capacity for **up to another $100M**, advancing capital against recurring creator platform revenue — Roblox, Spotify, YouTube, TikTok Shop — plus traditional business loans. The underwriting thesis: "cult-like" audiences — unusually loyal, predictable, engaged audiences with strong IP; engagement quality, IP strength, revenue reliability as factors, not raw follower counts. The maturity signal: first creators proved audience, then revenue, now *financeability* — the creator economy shifting from sponsorship income to underwritable cash flow. Capital can fund IP acquisition/creation (licensing, merch, catalogs), not just operations; infrastructure demand follows (reputation, completed work, payment history become underwriting data).
4. **Protocol-native funding + agent-as-cryptographic-peer.** OpenSats announced **$36.6M in Bitcoin to 413 grantees across 40+ countries** (Sept 7, 2026) with a **100% pass-through model** (operating costs covered separately; Q2 2026: 36 BTC for $12.51 in fees; monthly disbursements ~$1M) and the **Nostr Fund** as one of two primary pools; major donors Jack Dorsey's #startsmall + Reynolds Foundation. Adjacent: Dorsey's **$10M to "and Other Stuff"** (May 2026 collective: Nostr, ActivityPub, Cashu; "community of hackers" rejecting VC structures; ethical guidelines for privacy/interoperability/transparency). Separately, Buzz on Nostr (NostrMag analysis, Sept 8) holds AI agents as **full network members with their own keypairs** — every message, code review, and workflow step a signed event in the same log as human teammates; BuilderBot internally executes 200K+ ops/day, merges 1,500+ PRs/week, ~15% of production code changes (vendor-internal, unaudited). The protocol + funding + identity layer is a single coherent posture: open protocols as agent infrastructure, funded by protocol-native grants rather than VC or corporate patronage.
5. **ARR tracker gains two lanes (refresh).** The `china-open-source-llm-arr-tracker` gains two lanes: (a) the **funding-source column gains its sixth lane** — pass-through public-goods grants (OpenSats), distinct from endowments, co-ops, patronage, corporate sponsorship, and vendor-funded agentic support because it is breadth-first, protocol-native, and VC-structure-averse; (b) the **identity column gains its first open-protocol lane**: agent-as-cryptographic-peer. Every lab row now carries five tags — mechanism + margin-quality + weights-verification + funding-source + identity-verification — and the identity-verification column becomes load-bearing as the mechanism lanes (inference-revenue, license-toll, cloud-revshare, vertical-agent ARR, consent-corpus barter, sovereign-full-stack) all eventually need to attribute agent traffic on open protocols rather than platform registries.
