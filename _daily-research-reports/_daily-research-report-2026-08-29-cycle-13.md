## Research Phase Findings by Domain

### 1. Agent Economy / AI Agents and Workflows
- **ERC-8004 Empirical Study** — **NOVEL, HIGH IMPACT.** "Can Trustless Agents Be Trusted?" (Xiong et al., Imperial College/Ohio State/Bristol/CSIRO/Manchester, arXiv:2606.26028v2). First cross-chain empirical study: 170k+ agents, Ethereum/BSC/Base, through May 13, 2026. Reputation Registry fails all four necessary trust-signal conditions (C1 commensurability — untyped value field; C2 robustness — mean aggregator breakdown point zero, one crafted feedback flips any score; C3 groundedness — 98.7–100% of feedback lacks payment proof or task linkage; C4 economics — attack cost $0.0027–$0.055 vs median value at stake $0.70, 259× gap). Sybil reviewers: 73.5%/59.2%/90.6% by chain; removing them leaves 15.8%/77.9%/86.8% of rated agents with no valid feedback. Seven concrete protocol recommendations. **New skill: `erc8004-empirical-trust-reality-check-2026`** (ai-agents-and-workflows). Directly stress-tests cycle 12's agentic-credit-infrastructure convergence.
- **Agent credit bureau second wave** (incremental to convergence skill): AgentTrust (MIT, machine-native credit bureau on Base, x402-metered $0.01/$0.05 queries), Lookout (Celo+Base, ZK human verification via Self Protocol +15, credit-bureau model, no target cooperation), Cred402 (Casper, x402-receipt → credit line, "agents from tools into financeable economic actors"), Tessera (HashKey Chain, 0–1000 scores, under-collateralized lending, AP2 mandate chains), Ledgermind/ai-agent-credit-dashboard (Apache 2.0, credit-as-financial-primitive with labor market, Proving Ground grader≠solver), openclaw-agent-reputation (MIT, FICO-style 300–850 soulbound). Plus **AgentKarma archived June 2026** — the first agent credit bureau mortality signal (stopped indexing; 30,815 wallets final snapshot).
- **TrustScoreAgent** (Apache 2.0): service-side (not wallet-side) reputation registry with Beta reputation + EigenTrust, Merkle audit trail, Ed25519 receipt verification; honest Phase-1 trust-model disclosure (single operator, self-asserted agent identity).

### 3. Developer Experience
- **JetBrains HAX study (ICSE 2026)** — **NOVEL.** Two years of telemetry, 800 developers, 151.9M events: AI redistributes workflows in ways perception misses. Editing inversion (AI users +~100 deletions/month vs +7 non-users, while reporting no change); quality perception up while debugging behavior flat; context switching up while perceived down. **New skill: `hax-perception-behavior-gap-2026`** (developer-experience-and-flow).
- **2026 survey convergence wave** (incremental but convergent, captured as evidence inside two skills): JetBrains AI Pulse (10,013 devs; Claude Code 6× YoY work adoption, CSAT 91/NPS 54; "product excellence outweighs ecosystem lock-in"); Digital Applied Q1 (2,847 devs); **GitKraken State of AI** (554 devs — 84% feel faster, 39% of orgs measure nothing, 72% on belief not numbers; agentic 7.6%→28% in nine months); Sonar State of Code (1,149 — 96% don't fully trust AI code, 48% always check, toil changes flavor not volume); Halkwinds (758 orgs — 76% deployment, 34% audited impact, 66% no pre-baseline, 63% hardest ROI ever). Cross-referenced: no dedicated GitKraken/proof-gap skill exists; the `agent-experience-design-2026` skill touches GitKraken but not the proof-gap finding — captured within the HAX skill's evidence base.
- **DPTrainer (JetBrains, Aug 27)** — already captured (cycle 9, `dptrainer-drop-in-differential-privacy`). Confirmed current.

### 4. Cognitive Science / UX
- **Review overtakes writing** — **NOVEL.** Digital Applied Q1 2026: 11.4 hrs/week reviewing AI-generated code vs 9.8 writing — the primary AI workflow flipped. Review fatigue in 37% of write-ins; failure modes: merge under-reviewed or queue PRs indefinitely. Converging evidence: Sonar (38% say reviewing AI code takes MORE effort than human code; toil shifted to tech-debt management 44% + AI correction 25% for heavy users), GitClear churn/duplication, Halkwinds review-time budgets. **New skill: `review-overtakes-writing-threshold-2026`** (cognitive-science-and-ux).

### 5. Behavioral Psychology
- **Engagement-gated nudge effectiveness** — **NOVEL.** Two 2026 field studies: (a) Recife school-choice RCT (IDB, Nov 2025) — null ITT but strong complier effects; 99% single-login, 3-minute sessions, 75.6% pre-decided — timing/engagement gates, not nudge quality; (b) Lipman et al. doubly randomized trial (Behavioural Public Policy, Mar 2026, n=839) — self-selected interventions beat control while assigned ones didn't; alignment (not the act of choosing) partly drives it. Unified four-gate framework (timing → engagement → receptivity → autonomy). **New skill: `engagement-gated-nudge-effectiveness-2026`** (behavioral-psychology-and-nudging). Complements existing `choice-architecture-occupational-decisions` (Yousty) without duplicating it.

### 6. Privacy-First AI
- **Clifti-GPT** — **NOVEL.** SMPC-protected federated fine-tuning + **transferable inference** (exchange secure voting statistics, not embeddings) for foundation models on clinical single-cell data; within 4% of centralized; 99% in ≤2 rounds; 30 clients <2% loss; Apache 2.0. The secure-KNN overhead cliff (11k–70k×) is the practical budget. **New skill: `clifti-gpt-federated-transferable-inference`** (privacy-and-trust).
- **SSTQ** — **NOVEL (with caveats).** Privacy+compression in one quantization step: O(4^b)→O(2^b) error (256× at 8 bits); ~20+b bits/client/round for d=1M. Unreviewed, two image datasets only, no code yet. **New skill: `sstq-privacy-compression-co-design`** (privacy-and-trust).
- Incremental: FedGSA (Grassmann-manifold DP federated LoRA — complements FFA-LoRA/FedSVD line, already represented via fedgsa skill in library); EFedProx (blockchain-verified multilevel DP for medical FL — domain-specific); Federated LLM production guide (FedLoRA payload math, framework comparison — incremental to existing FL skills).

## Cross-Domain Observations

### The Re-Centralization Pattern (August 2026)
Three independent events push the same direction: metered licenses tax downstream revenue (Kimi K3/Qwen3.8-Max), the commons gets acquired (Nvidia–HF), and the trust layer's openness proves gamed at scale (ERC-8004 Sybils). The counter-moves are also converging: permissive-license alternatives (DeepSeek MIT), sovereign mirrors/registries, verified-not-self-reported trust designs (grader≠solver, staked juries, Merkle-audited receipts), and SMPC/DP layers keeping computation local. A-Tech's sovereignty thesis has never had better empirical support — or more urgent timing.

### The Perception-Behavior Divergence as 2026's Meta-Finding
HAX (editing inversion, quality perception), GitKraken (84% feel / 72% can't prove), Sonar (96% distrust yet 48% check), METR (believed faster, measured slower) — the same structure appears in DevEx and in ERC-8004 (self-reported feedback vs verified interaction). The trust-by-verification principle is now the connective tissue between agent economics and developer measurement. Cycle 12's hunch was right; this cycle quantified it in both domains.

### The Engagement Gate Unifies Nudge and Adoption Findings
Recife's null-ITT/strong-complier split and the Colombia ICFES pop-up experiments (519k students: engagement up 3–5×, enrollment effects limited) mirror the DevEx adoption wave (76–96% adoption, 34–20% measurable impact). In both domains, average effects are engagement-weighted artifacts; design must target the gates (timing, engagement, receptivity, autonomy) and measurement must separate deployment effects from mechanism effects.
