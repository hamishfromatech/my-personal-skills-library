## 1. Research Summary

Third run of cycle 22. The window (Sept 4–11) produced three theme clusters: (1) **the enterprise cost-control turn meets the fine-tuning wedge** — Ramp's September AI Index (Sept 10) showed median per-employee AI spend falling 9.7% with frontier-model share of enterprise tokens dropping 53%→45% in a month, the same week Abacus.AI shipped the Smaug fine-tuned open-weight line (Sept 10) pricing self-hosted agentic inference at 10-100× below frontier cost — one story: enterprise buyers are squeezing the token bill exactly as a new open-weight product pattern arrives to absorb the volume; (2) **open-source governance moved from opinion to evidence** — the CNCF Technical Oversight Committee's 72-project data review (Aug 26: org-balanced voting as the strongest diversity predictor, 2.07× graduation multiplier) crossed with the CHASE'26 role-drift study (the Maintainer Paradox: 50 of 133 documented roles are composite maintainers, the role meant to distribute power centralizing it) and the ACM Hyperlayered Hypertext framework (Sept 6: agent document work must attach to the substrate, not substitute it); and (3) **the interaction-layer and agent-skills design turn** — the Cognitive Design Layer's eighteen offload principles (Feb 2026) and the first controlled progressive-disclosure-for-agents evidence (arXiv:2607.17598 + the preregistered 709-page wiki ablation) converge on one rule: structure buys context and control, not intelligence — flat skill packs and attached document layers beat deep hierarchies and substituted answers. Financial freedom contributes the Treasury-buyback mechanism lesson (Kiyosaki's "QE" label vs the actual debt-refinancing mechanism). All 6 selections passed grep dedup; 3 refreshes landed in place.
