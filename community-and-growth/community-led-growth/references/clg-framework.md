# Community-Led Growth (CLG) Framework Reference

## The Growth Loop in Detail

### Stage 1: Engagement
**Goal:** Get community members actively participating

**Tactics:**
- Welcome challenges: "Introduce yourself and share your AI project"
- Daily/weekly discussion prompts
- "Show and Tell" sessions where members demo their work
- Q&A threads with experts

**A-Tech Application:**
- Builder's Club "Monday Motivation" threads
- "What's your local AI setup?" weekly showcase
- A-Coder "Code Challenge of the Week"

### Stage 2: Feedback
**Goal:** Capture and act on community input

**Tactics:**
- Public feature voting boards
- "What's frustrating you?" surveys
- Bug bounty programs
- "Rate this feature" after releases

**A-Tech Application:**
- A-Coder feature request board with upvoting
- "Be Practical" playbook topic suggestions
- Monthly "What should we build next?" polls

### Stage 3: Iteration
**Goal:** Show the community their input matters

**Tactics:**
- Public roadmap with status updates
- "You asked, we built" release notes
- Behind-the-scenes development updates
- Community tester programs

**A-Tech Application:**
- Monthly "Community Impact Report" showing implemented suggestions
- Beta testing group for A-Coder updates
- "Reader's Choice" playbook topics

### Stage 4: Advocacy
**Goal:** Turn satisfied members into promoters

**Tactics:**
- Referral programs with rewards
- User-generated content campaigns
- Testimonial collection
- Ambassador programs

**A-Tech Application:**
- "Share Your AI Story" campaign
- Review incentives (bonus content for Amazon reviews)
- Builder Ambassador tier with exclusive perks

### Stage 5: Renewed Engagement
**Goal:** Complete the loop with new members

**Tactics:**
- Onboarding new members with community stories
- Highlighting advocate success stories
- Creating "path to advocacy" journey maps
- Celebrating community milestones

**A-Tech Application:**
- "How I Became an AI Owner" onboarding series
- Member milestone celebrations (100th builder, etc.)
- "From User to Contributor" journey maps

## Community Engagement Formulas

### The 1-9-90 Rule
In any community:
- **1%** create content
- **9%** interact with content
- **90%** lurk (observe only)

**Strategy:** Focus on converting lurkers to interactors, then interactors to creators.

### Time-to-First-Value (TTFV)
The faster a new member experiences value, the more likely they'll stay.

**A-Tech Tactics:**
- Day 1: Welcome guide + starter template
- Week 1: First "win" (simple project completed)
- Month 1: First contribution or meaningful interaction

### Community Health Score
Track these metrics to measure health:

1. **Activity Rate:** % of members active in last 30 days
2. **Contribution Rate:** % of active members who created content
3. **Response Time:** Average time to first response in support threads
4. **Retention Rate:** % of members still active after 90 days
5. **Advocacy Rate:** % of members who referred someone

## Moderation & Governance

### Community Guidelines Template
```
1. Be Respectful: Disagree with ideas, not people
2. Share Generously: Your knowledge helps everyone grow
3. Protect Privacy: No sharing of personal/sensitive data
4. Stay On-Topic: Use appropriate channels
5. Give Credit: Acknowledge others' contributions
6. Report Issues: Help us keep the community safe
```

### Escalation Path
1. Friendly reminder (private message)
2. Warning (public, if needed)
3. Temporary mute
4. Permanent removal

### Transparency Practices
- Publish moderation decisions (when appropriate)
- Regular community surveys on culture
- Open discussion about community direction
- Community-elected moderators (for larger communities)
